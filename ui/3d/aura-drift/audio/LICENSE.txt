AuraGo Aura Drift sound pack

Most files in this pack are original work for AuraGo, generated from code by
scripts/build-aura-drift-audio.mjs (copyright (c) 2026 AuraGo contributors, MIT licence):
all engines, all music, and every sound group that is not listed below.

The sound groups listed below are cut from third-party recordings that are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Attribution is not required; it is given here as a courtesy. The recordings were trimmed,
looped with a short crossfade where the group is a loop, converted to mono 44.1 kHz,
peak-normalised and encoded to Ogg Vorbis. The originals are not part of this repository;
assets/aura-drift/audio/sources.json pins every download by URL, size and SHA-256, and
manifest.json records the source and archive member of every file.
