# Runenpfad — Virtual Desktop 2D Action-Platformer

## Purpose

Offline 2D action-platformer game (`runenpfad`, category `games`) for the AuraGo Virtual Desktop with a 25-level campaign across five biomes, multi-phase world bosses with telegraphed attack patterns, two playable heroes, persistent Rune Workshop upgrades, per-level Rune Trials, an Endless Rune Tower mode, parallax biome backdrops, adaptive procedural music and an optional WebGL2 post-processing stage. Interiors also hold switchable and movable room things, six room guardians, the ten journal pages of Edda's chronicle with the observatory chapter, a rune dice table and a task board.

Round 3 adds five enemy kinds (`spitter`, `swooper`, `shielded`, `phaser`, `brute`), stomping, four rune power-ups (`fire`, `frost`, `storm`, `magnet`), three new zones (`geyser_field`, `rune_gate`, `flame_jets`), a Codex-generated art atlas with sprite FX and large boss art, and adaptive camera zoom.

Round 4 adds swinging ropes (`swing_gorge`), three puzzle types (`rune_sequence` memory at a seer stone, `lever_bridge`, timed `ritual_chain`), dragon strafe set pieces with stone shelters (`dragon_strafe`), `drakeling` enemies and ember/frost dragon bosses in the endless tower.

NPC encounters add six recurring characters (`witch`, `wizard`, `merchant`, `chief`, `seer`, `alchemist`) who stand at camps in campaign levels and endless floors. The hero talks to them through offline scripted choice dialogues (16 locales), trades with them, and builds reputation, remembered flags and quests that persist in the profile. A character can also be fought in a real-time close-up duel. Each world carries a 2–3 meeting arc, and level 25 holds the finale.

Round 5 adds enterable houses and caves (top-down interiors with free 8-direction movement, real-time combat, chests, rest spots and NPC dialogue), the village hub Pathrest (campaign stage id 0, a peaceful street with five doors and its own folk), resident NPCs with dialogue graphs, 13 story cutscenes (27 pixel-art panels) in a full-screen overlay, place and story music, and a chronicle tab in the world map.

Gauntlets add one harder section before the key sanctum or boss arena of every campaign level. There are 12 variants: `thorn_hop`, `crumble_steps`, `spike_tunnel`, `pendulum_bridge`, `trap_isle`, `guarded_isle`, `crumble_chain`, `ferry`, `cannon_islands`, `cannon_crossfire`, `jet_islands` and `conveyor_gauntlet`. Each is a timing-based run of pit hazards, spike traps, crumbling ledges, a pendulum, moving platforms, cannons, flame jets, conveyor belts or two guards, with its own par-time bonus. The hub and endless floors have none.

The play-feel round adds variable jump height, camera look-ahead, hitstop on hits, gamepad support, volume sliders and a checkpoint before every campaign gauntlet that re-arms the revive. It also adds a loudness-balanced sound mix with new UI and movement sounds, coloured light, iris transitions, painted chains, conveyors, projectiles and warning rings.

- **Enemy event payloads**: Enemy-originated events carry `defId` plus `x`/`y`. Audio identifies enemies by `defId` and pans by `x`. `enemy_attack.kind` is `shot`, `lunge`, `dive` (crystal bat), `lob` (sporecap) or `fireball` (drakeling and dragon boss). Hazard kinds add `poison`, `flame_jet` and `dragonfire`. `boss_intro`, `boss_phase` and `boss_defeat` carry `defId`. Duel events carry no `x`: the duel is its own arena, and audio would pan it by the world camera.
- **Stomp**: Falling onto an enemy's top (`vy > 120`, not phased) deals 1 damage (2 with the fire rune) and bounces the hero. The bounce is higher while jump is held (`app.js` `readInput` reports held `jump`). Bosses take at most one stomp per 0.6 s.
- **Shielded and phased enemies**: A shielded scarab blocks hits from the side it faces. Rear hits, stomps and the knight's air slam pierce. Phased frost wraiths are intangible.
- **Camera**: Adaptive zoom `clamp(viewH/576, 1, 2)` (about 12 tiles), multiplied by 0.86 while a boss is alive, clamped to `[0.85, 2]`. The follow comes from `camera-rig.js`. The hero sits at 40 % of the view width from the trailing edge, so 60 % lies ahead in the facing direction. The lead side changes only after the hero has faced the new way for 0.35 s or moved 48 px that way, then eases over at rate 2.2 per second. X follows at rate `1 - exp(-dt * 10.5)`. The standing anchor is the hero at 62 % of the view height, and the target height follows where the hero last stood or climbed. In the air the camera keeps that height, unless the hero leaves the band between 22 % and 74 % of the view height or falls faster than 520 px/s; then it follows. Y follows at rate `1 - exp(-dt * 9)`. The camera snaps without easing on `reset()` and when the hero moved more than 400 px in x or y since the last update. The clamps apply to the rig output as before. World layers draw under the zoomed transform. Sky, parallax and weather draw in screen space. The camera never shows below the stage bottom; on the hub (`stage.hub`) the horizontal clamp keeps a one-tile margin at both street ends (`view.js`). Positions handed to postfx and lighting are converted to screen space. `getCamera()` and the frame view expose world extents, which audio panning depends on.
- **Variable jump** (`sim.js`, `app.js`): `readInput` sets `input.jumpCut: true`, and the sim cuts a jump only while `input.jumpCut` is truthy. Test bots that pass no `jumpCut` keep the full height. Each launch (ground, coyote and air jump) sets `player.jumpRising = true` and `player.jumpVyMark` to the launch speed. While `vy` still equals `jumpVyMark` and is negative, the jump keeps `jumpRising`. Any other change to `vy` (spring, geyser, updraft, stomp, hurt, swing release) clears it, and the swing, dash and ladder or rope branches clear it too. The cut applies when `input.jump` is not held, `jumpRising` is set and `vy` is faster than `0.45 × jumpVel` upwards: `vy` is set to `-0.45 × jumpVel` and the flag clears. A held jump keeps the old arc, and the hitstop branch leaves the jump state alone.
- **Hitstop** (`combat.js`, `sim.js`): a melee hit that takes hp from a living enemy freezes the world for 0.03 s (0.04 s when the target is a boss), once per swing. A hero projectile that damages without killing freezes it for 0.015 s. A non-lethal hit on the hero freezes it for 0.05 s when the stage is still `playing` and no revive happened. Kills keep their existing freezes (0.045 s, or 0.12 s for a boss), and a slam landing keeps 0.06 s. Blocked hits and crates freeze nothing. In the freeze branch of `sim.js`, a jump press sets `p.jumpBuffer = 0.11`, so a jump pressed during a freeze fires when the freeze ends. The freeze keeps resolving attack, heal and pickups.
- **Iris transitions** (`feel-fx.js`, `view.js`): `drawScreen` draws the iris from `finishFrame`, after the screen flashes and before `postfx.render`, so it is the top layer on both paths. The duel and interior branches do not draw it. Stage start: the iris opens from the hero over 0.55 s of simulation time, so pause and cutscenes hold it. `player_defeat`: the iris closes toward the hero over 0.9 s of frame time and holds a 30 px ring at the hero, scaled by zoom. `portal_win`: the iris closes on the portal over 0.7 s of frame time. Reduced motion: a plain alpha fade in each case. `reset(sim)`, called when the stage changes, clears the defeat and win irises and starts the stage-start iris. Any step while `sim.state` is `'playing'` clears the defeat and win irises, and a step while it is not `'playing'` ends the stage-start iris.
- **Coloured light** (`atmosphere.js`, `view.js`, `decor.js`, `stage-art.js`, `set-pieces.js`): a light entry may carry `color: '#rrggbb'` (six hex digits; other forms are ignored) and `tintScale` (default 1). Lights without a `color` add no tint. After the light canvas is drawn, `paintLighting` adds one additive radial tint per coloured light, with radius 0.9 × the light radius and alpha `0.16 × tintScale × clamp(intensity × flicker, 0, 1)`, scaled by `clamp((ambient - 0.1) / 0.35, 0, 1)`, so bright biomes (ambient 0.1 or lower) show none. The hero light takes `biome.light.color` with `tintScale` 0.5. The GPU path and the canvas fallback share this code.
- **Module load order**: `module-loader.js` (the `runenpfad` entry) is the source of truth. The order is `biomes` → `fx` → `physics` → `behaviors` → `bosses` → `variety` → `profile` → `runes` → `zones` → `zones-extra` → `gauntlets` → `swing` → `puzzles` → `dragons` → `npcs` → `npc-story` → `npc-story-late` → `dialogue` → `trade` → `duel-patterns` → `duel` → `place-rooms` → `interior-mobs` → `interior-bosses` → `interior-things` → `interior` → `places` → `folk` → `folk-story` → `journal-story` → `village-rooms` → `place-rooms-deep` → `village` → `cutscenes` → `chronicle` → `dice` → `bounties` → `levels` → `combat` → `sim` → `audio-dsp` → `sfx` → `sfx-rooms` → `audio-mix` → `audio` → `music-voices` → `music` → `music-scores` → `duel-music` → `place-music` → `story-music` → `backdrops` → `backdrops-biomes` → `parallax-art` → `atmosphere` → `decor` → `art` → `props-art` → `set-pieces` → `actors` → `stage-art` → `npc-view` → `duel-view` → `place-view` → `interior-bake` → `interior-things-view` → `interior-view` → `cutscene-view` → `postfx` → `camera-rig` → `feel-fx` → `view` → `hud` → `dialogue-ui` → `worldmap-ui` → `cutscene-ui` → `games-ui` → `gamepad` → `app`. Each module reads its dependencies from the shared global scope at call time, so a new module must be placed after everything it reads. `place-music.js` registers into `RunenpfadMusic` at load, so it must follow `music.js`; `story-music.js` uses `RunenpfadPlaceMusic.tools` and must follow `place-music.js`.
- **Music**: Each boss theme has its own lead instrument: boar horn with war drums (boar), goblin dark bells with plucked strings (goblin), desert orb low reed over a drone (desert), glacier glass bells (glacier), and a citadel half-time machine groove with fuzz bass (citadel). Boss themes stay minor or phrygian. The sim test forbids a major third in `boss_desert`. Duels play one loop per character (`duel_<npc>`, selected by `RunenpfadDuelMusic.themeFor` while `sim.encounter` is a duel); each loop is boss-style and has no major third. Round 5 themes: `village` (ionian, 104 bpm), `in_cozy` (ionian, 132 bpm, tavern and houses), `in_cave` (phrygian, 54 bpm) from `place-music.js`; `story_dawn` (ionian, 66 bpm, harp and flute/celesta), `story_dark` (locrian, 56 bpm, no major third) and `story_triumph` (ionian, 120 bpm) from `story-music.js`. All five biomes carry 5 distinct stage suites each (25 total in `music-scores.js` with `stageTempoMul`), selected via `stageInWorld`-aligned `stageSeed`; all biomes, world bosses, duels and `in_cave` include a `'drama'` layer driven by `intensityFor(sim)` (dragons, rituals, resonance timers, low HP, interior fights, combat swarms, boss/duel Phase 2 enraged, heroic surges) through a fast-attack/hold/release envelope, dynamic bar tempo push, `master.setDrama` EQ/wet/saturation scaling and dramatic stingers (`music-voices.js`, `VOICE_CAP = 48`). A theme may carry a hand-written `compose(theme, layer, bar, seed)` hook; `music.js` calls it instead of `music-scores.js` / stock fallback. `RunenpfadPlaceMusic.themeFor(sim)`: inside an interior the room's `music` (fallback `in_cozy`), on the hub `village`, otherwise null; `music.js` checks duel first, then place. `music.setOverride(themeId | null)` plays the given theme regardless of the simulation (not saved; a timer pumps it every 100 ms); the cutscene overlay sets the scene's theme on open and each scene change and clears it on close. Keep the music and audio controllers within the 1100-line desktop budget.
- **Observatory and guardian music**: `in_obs` is D lydian at 58 bpm with a ticking pulse, glass and celesta over a low drone, and a `'drama'` layer for guardian fights (`composeObs` in `place-music.js`). `PlaceMusic.themeFor` returns the room's `music` inside an interior, so the observatory plays `in_obs` and the cave rooms `in_cave`. While a guardian is alive in the room, `intensityFor` returns `boss: true`, `danger` of at least 0.6, `drama` 1 and scene `combat` (`enraged` stays false), and the place theme keeps playing through the fight.
- **Pause dialog order**: `resume`, `restart`, `sound`, `music`, `gfx`, `reset`. The browser test clicks the first `aria-pressed` button, so keep `resume` first and keep the toggles in this order. `gfx` is hidden while no GPU stage is attached and the preference is `auto`. Inside an interior `hud.paintLegend(controls, inside)` swaps the key legend rows. Round 9: `hud.createPause` builds the dialog and returns its elements to `app.js`; the two volume rows come after the action buttons (see Volume sliders). The button order is unchanged.
- **Gamepad** (`gamepad.js`, `app.js`): `poll()` reads the first pad with the `standard` mapping, and `app.js` calls it once per frame, also while paused or on a round card. The D-pad (buttons 12 to 15) and the left stick map to `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight` and `ArrowDown`, with dead zone 0.35. `ArrowUp` needs a mostly vertical push beyond 0.7. A → `Space`, X → `KeyJ`, B → `KeyK`, Y → `KeyQ`, LB and RB → `KeyE`, Start → `KeyP`. Only changes are sent. `app.js` turns each one into a keydown or keyup event on the shell element, which bubbles to the window handlers. While a dialog is open, the D-pad moves focus among its enabled buttons and inputs (range inputs move by their step), A clicks the focused element, B sends `Escape` and Start sends `KeyP`. On a round card, B sends `Escape`. `onEvents` rumbles through `vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble', …)` for `player_hurt` (strength 0.6, 140 ms) and for `slam_land` and `boss_slam` (strength 0.8, 180 ms), never under reduced motion. `seen()` is true once a pad has connected, and the pause legend then shows `runenpfad.legend.gamepad`.
- **Volume sliders** (`hud.js`, `app.js`, `profile.js`): the pause dialog has two `input type="range"` rows, labelled `runenpfad.pause.sfx_volume` and `runenpfad.pause.music_volume`, from 0 to 100 in steps of 5. `input` applies the value at once through `audio.setSfxVolume` or `audio.setMusicVolume` (via `applyVolumes()`), and `change` saves the profile. `applyVolumes()` also runs right after the audio controller is created, so saved values apply from the start. The values are `profile.sfxVol` and `profile.musicVol` (see Save integrity).
- **Win card and HUD reactions** (`hud.js`): on a win, the earned stars light up one after another (the first after 250 ms, then every 320 ms) with `ui_star_<n>`. The goal rows and plaques fade in after them, and a newly unlocked level plays `ui_unlock`. Reduced motion shows everything at once; the cues keep the same timing. `cancelWin()` clears the pending timers, and `app.js` calls it when a stage starts. `hud.onEvents` bumps the coin chip on `pickup_coin`, shakes the hearts on `player_hurt` and pulses them on `heart_beat`. These pulses are skipped under reduced motion. The CSS uses transitions and classes only (`is-pending`, `is-bump`, `is-shake`, `is-beat`).
- **Save integrity**: `importProfile` validates `v === 1` and clamps `unlockedLevel`, `endlessBestFloor`, `coins`, `totalScore`, `upgrades`, `starsByLevel`, `bestTimeByLevel`, and the `music` (boolean, default on) and `gfx` (`auto` or `canvas`) preferences, and `sfxVol` and `musicVol` (numbers 0..1 rounded to 0.05, default 1, invalid values become 1). Invalid saves fall back cleanly to defaults. The save format stays `aurago.runenpfad.v1`. In `app.js`, `rejectedRaw` is declared before the first `readProfile()` call: `readProfile` assigns it, so a later `let` throws inside the `try` and every saved profile is replaced by the defaults. `TestRunenpfadRegistration` checks the app registration (id, category, runtime, `open_maximized`, `logo_path`), the icon file, and that `module-loader.js` and the other listed files contain their expected markers, including each runenpfad module path. It checks that the paths are present, not their order. The browser test reopens the app and expects the unlocked stage and the bought upgrade.
- **Story save caps**: `importProfile` clamps the story fields. `rep[faction]` is −5..5 (unknown factions dropped). `npcFlags` keys must match `FLAG_RE` (`/^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,15}\.[a-z0-9_]{1,23}$/`), values are 0..99, zeros are dropped, and the first 96 sorted keys are kept (`setFlag` refuses a new key beyond 96). `inventory` accepts only `STORY_ITEMS` ids (caps: `glow_mushroom`, `sun_shard`, `gear_part` 9; `hex_charm` 1; `lucky_amulet`, `discount_token` 3; `rune_attune` 2). `quests.active` (max 8) and `quests.done` (max 16) take only `QUEST_IDS`, deduplicated, with `done` winning over `active`. A missing or invalid block falls back to its default. `app.js` ignores raw saves over 16384 characters, so new collections must stay capped.
- **Round 5 save fields**: `loot` is an array of strings matching `LOOT_RE` (`/^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,23}$/`), deduplicated, sorted, at most 48 entries. It records campaign chests that were paid (`takeLoot`). `seen` is an array matching `SEEN_RE` (`/^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,15}$/`), deduplicated, sorted, at most 24 entries; it lists the cutscene ids already played. Both default to `[]` and both are clamped by `importProfile`. Endless and repeat chests never write `loot`.
- **Encounter state**: `sim.encounter = null | {kind: 'dialogue'|'duel', npcRef, npcId, nodeId, panel: 'talk'|'trade', returnNode, seq, openedTick, duel?}` exists only at runtime. `seq` changes on every node or panel change and is the dialogue window's re-render key. `sim.mode` stays `'campaign'|'endless'` (stage restart, win card and HUD depend on it); never reuse it for an encounter or an interior. `sim.state` stays `'playing'` during an encounter, so `isPaused()` and the music state logic are unchanged.
- **Interior state and tick gate**: `sim.interior` is `null` or the interior object (below). It is runtime only and never saved. `sim.uiRequest` is `null | 'workshop' | 'chronicle' | 'worldmap'`, set by the DOM-free core (dialogue effect `ui`, the village arch) and consumed and cleared by `app.js` after the step. `stepOneTick` order: `sim.state !== 'playing'` returns; if `sim.interior` and no `sim.encounter`, `RunenpfadPlaces.step` runs and the tick ends; then `RunenpfadNpcs.hold` (a dialogue opened inside an interior is held here); then `RunenpfadPlaces.tryEnter` (door proximity and the enter key); then the world. While an interior is open the level clock, runes, enemies and hazards are frozen. Stage arrays: `stage.doors[]` (always present, from `extras.doors`), `stage.placeState` and `stage.placesReady` (runtime, per stage run), `stage.hub` and `stage.barks[]` on the hub.
- **Doors** (`stage.doors[]`): `{ id: 'door_<n>', placeId, kind: 'house'|'cave'|'arch', x, y, w: 56, zoneX0, zoneX1, near, cool, facade: { clip, h, dx } }`. `x` is the door centre and `y` the ground line in px. `facade.h` is the draw height and `facade.dx` the horizontal offset of the facade centre from the door centre. `kind: 'arch'` exists only on the hub and sets `sim.uiRequest = 'worldmap'` instead of entering. `tryEnter` sets `door.near` when the hero is within 40 px horizontally, on the ground and within 8 px vertically; `place_near` is emitted once per approach and the hint `runenpfad.notice.place_hint` replaces `sim.notice` while a door is near. Entering needs `input.talkPressed` (`'e'`, or `'up'` on the ground, off ladders and ropes, `|vx| < 60`); the enter clears the jump and attack edges and zeroes `vx`. `door.cool` (0.6 s after leaving) blocks immediate re-entry.
- **Place zone** (`places.js`): `build` lays `ZONE_TILES = 9` tiles of flat ground, pushes one door at the zone centre and returns 9. No enemies, hazards or rng draws. An unknown place id builds nothing and returns 0. `resolve` (once per stage run) moves enemies spawned inside a door zone outward and clamps patrol bounds. Entering creates the interior (`RunenpfadInterior.create`), sets `sim.interior` and emits `place_enter`. `leave(sim, reason)` clears `sim.interior`, puts the hero on the door (`player.x = door.x`, velocity zero), sets `door.cool = 0.6` and emits `place_leave`. On `'ko'` the hero keeps 1 hp, gets `invulnTimer = 2`, the notice `runenpfad.notice.place_ko` and the event `place_ko`. Room state is not copied on leave: `stage.placeState[placeId] = { cleared, sold, opened, used }` is written as each change happens and stays until the stage restarts. `locked(sim)` is true while an interior is open (app.js uses it like `duelLocked()`). `sealed(sim, placeId)` is true while the place's `requires.flag` is unset; a sealed door shows the notice `runenpfad.notice.sealed`, emits `in_locked`, does not enter and waits 0.5 s before the next try. The observatory is the only sealed place (`requires: { flag: 'obs.open' }`).
- **Loot** (`takeLoot`): a chest pays `coins` through `RunenpfadNpcs.addCoins`, `give` through `RunenpfadDialogue.applyEffects`, `item` through `RunenpfadNpcs.addItem`, and sets `sim.storyDirty`. A non-repeat chest id is added to `profile.loot` (sorted, at most 48). Endless chests (`repeat`) pay 15 coins per open and are remembered only in `placeState.opened`.
- **Interior rooms** (`place-rooms.js`): `PLACES[id] = { id, nameKey: 'runenpfad.place.<id>', kind: 'house'|'cave', entry, facade: { clip, h, dx }, rooms, world: 0..4|null }`. `ROOMS[roomId] = { theme: 'wood'|'cave'|'tomb'|'ice'|'works', floor, floorAlt, wall, dark, music: 'in_cozy'|'in_cave'|'in_obs' (default `in_cozy`), map, things, exits, spawn } and the optional `slippery`, `orders` and `guardian` (see Interior things)`. Map characters: `#` wall, space void (both solid), `.` floor, `,` alternate floor, any other character a marker on a floor cell (one instance per cell). Rooms are 10 to 22 cells wide and 8 to 14 high. `things` entries carry one kind and an `at` marker: `prop` (`'tdp-<set>.p<n>'`, optional `w`, `h`, `solid`, `layer` `'obj'|'floor'`, `light`, `lit` (a dialogue condition that switches the light on)), `npc` (with `meeting` and an optional `if` condition; an NPC id missing from `RunenpfadNpcs.NPCS` is skipped, an NPC without a graph is ambient), `mob`, `chest` (`loot`, `locked: 'clear'|null`, `repeat`), `pickup` (`heart|coin|potion`), `light`, `rest` (heals 1 heart once per stage run; key in `placeState.used`). `PROP_DEFS` covers the 112 prop clips (see the `place-rooms.js` bullet under Ownership). The kinds of Interior things are valid in `things` too. `layout(roomId, sim)` is pure and returns a fresh parse; `CELL` is 48.
- **Place validator** (`RunenpfadPlaceRooms.validate()`, returns problem strings, empty = fine): equal row lengths; room size 10–22 × 8–14; every marker used by `things`, `exits` or `spawn` exists; every exit target and arrival marker exists; props stay inside the map and off walls; every prop clip exists in `PROP_DEFS`; one kind per thing; NPCs need a meeting id and, when `RunenpfadFolk` is loaded, a `SPRITES` entry; mobs are keys of `RunenpfadInteriorMobs.DEFS` (the seven normal kinds and the six guardians) and at least 4 cells from every entry; chests have unique loot ids matching `LOOT_RE`, a valid `give` and known `item` ids, and `locked: 'clear'` needs a mob in the room; NPC and chest cells must touch a reachable cell; mob, pickup, rest and exit cells must be reachable from an entry by flood fill; every place has an entry room with a spawn and an `out` exit, and every room of a place is reachable from the entry; the kind is `house` or `cave`; the facade clip is set.
- **Thing validator** (`place-rooms.js`, `validateThings`): the ids of plates, levers, braziers and gates match `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}$`; each id a condition names exists as a plate, lever or brazier of the room, and `lit` names a brazier set; `order` names a room `orders` set whose braziers all exist; `clear` needs a mob in the room; `pages` is 1 to 10; `flips` name levers; spikes need `up + 0.5 <= period - 0.6`; dart directions are `left`, `right`, `up` or `down`, with `every >= 1`; a page number is placed once across all rooms; sign nodes match `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$`; uses are `board`, `core` or `dice:<id>`; a block cell needs a free pair of opposite neighbours; a guardian needs a `mob` thing with its id. Thing kinds other than the seven original ones may not stand on an exit marker.
- **Deep rooms and the observatory** (`place-rooms-deep.js`): each cave place has a deep room behind its `X` exit: `beast_den_b` (`brood`), `crystal_grotto_b` (`weaver`), `sun_tomb_c` (`tombward`), `ice_cave_b` (`frostheart`, slippery) and `boiler_room_c` (`boilerward`). - **Duel rules** (`duel.js`, `duel-patterns.js`): Deterministic: randomness only through `Physics.rand`. Hero stats come from `sim.stats` (`maxHp = 8 + 2·maxHp`, `hit = 2 + damage`). A win sets `<npc>.beaten`, and its coins are paid only the first time. The special needs a rune active when the duel starts, and works once per duel. Potions and the heal key do nothing in a duel. Tells last at least 0.45 s in both phases, and each `duel_attack` follows its own `duel_tell`. Phase 2 starts once, at 50 % HP or below.
- **Provoke**: Attacking an NPC in front of them (`J`, `Z` or `Enter`, within 70 px) applies once per NPC per stage: the faction loses 1, the graph's `onAttack` effects run, `npc_provoke` is pushed, and the duel starts. Dialogue effects `provoke` and `duel` use the same path.
- **Camps**: The `npc_camp:<variant>` zone (`witch`, `merchant`, `chief`, `seer`, `wizard`, `alchemist`, two-NPC `a+b`, and `chief_gate`, which adds a portcullis column tied to the Krax gate) is 12 tiles of flat ground (13 with the gate). Camps place no enemies, no hazards and make no rng draws. `resolve()` runs once per stage (`stage.npcsReady`): it moves enemies spawned inside a camp outward, clamps patrol bounds, spawns quest items (`stage.questItems`), resets the per-stage trade flags and applies the citadel sabotage. - **Trade rules**: Price is `max(1, round(base × (1 − 0.06 × rep)))` for the seller's faction, so +5 gives 70 % and −5 gives 130 %; villagers with a `null` faction pay the base price. The merchant's haggle flag (`merchant.haggle`, cleared at stage start) takes a further 15 % off for the stage. Purchases spend `sim.profile.coins`, set `storyDirty`, and track stock per NPC per stage. Offers: `potion` (12, 2 per stage), `rune_fire|frost|storm|magnet` (20, 1 per stage), `meal` (8, 5 per stage, hp to max), `map_charm` (15, 1 per stage), and the stored items `lucky_amulet` (25), `discount_token` (30) and `rune_attune` (35), one each. Kobold fakes: the merchant at `kobolds ≤ −2` sells a fake with `Physics.rand` chance 0.35, which spends the coins and applies nothing. The reveal happens only when the dialogue closes (`RunenpfadTrade.onClose`), so an offer card never marks a fake. `map_charm` sets `sim.mapCharm` for the stage (reset at stage start); `npc-view.js` `drawCharm` then paints a glow ring on every uncollected gem on screen and an edge arrow toward each off-screen one (read-only, nothing in the bottom 40 px, steady under reduced motion). `rune_attune` is stored (cap 2); `runes.js` `grant` multiplies the next rune's duration by 1.5, consumes one through `addItem`, pushes `rune_attuned {x, y}` and the float `runenpfad.npc.float_attuned`.
- **Hero switch**: Switching heroes restarts the stage. Once the running stage has score or more than 20 s on the clock, `chooseHero` arms the choice and needs a second click within 4 s (top bar button and workshop cards share this). On the win card the switch keeps the card and applies to the next stage. `startStage`, `openLevels` and `openPause` disarm it. Pick buttons, restart and reset are disabled while `stageLocked()` (a duel or an interior is open).
- **Floating labels**: `sim.floatingTexts` entries are `{ x, y, text, color, timer, key?, suffix?, icon? }`. The DOM-free core never translates: `text` is the fallback, `key` a `runenpfad.*` translation key and `suffix` an untranslated tail such as ` 1/3`. The renderer draws `t(key) + suffix` when the key resolves, otherwise `text`, and paints `icon` (a `jump-and-run` asset id such as `heart`, `coin`, `potion`, `star`) behind the label. Every literal `key` in the core must exist in all 16 locales; the sim test scans `sim.js`, `combat.js`, `runes.js` and `zones-extra.js` for them. Literal keys in `npcs.js`, `trade.js`, `places.js` and `interior.js` should follow the same rule. Interiors keep their own float list (`interior.floats`), drawn in interior coordinates.

Change both together. `sim.newBest` is true only when a stored best time was beaten; a first clear sets the time without the tag. On level 25 the win card also shows `sim.epilogue` (the epilogue plaque).
- **Climb clips**: Ladder and rope climbing use `<hero>_climb_ladder` and `<hero>_climb_rope`. These are rear-view clips and are never flipped. The clock runs only while moving, reverses when descending, and falls back to the `<hero>_idle` clip when a clip is missing.

- **Puzzle solvability**: Puzzles must stay solvable for both heroes with unlimited retries. Oracles replay after a hit, every lever preset is checked by the sim test (toggles are self-inverse), ritual retries go through the plate, and a hero can always drop down when stairs sink. - **Gauntlets** (`gauntlets.js`; hooks in `levels.js`, `runes.js`, `npcs.js`):
  - Zone slot `gauntlet:<variant>` is the second-to-last zone of all 25 campaign levels, directly before `key_sanctum` or `boss_arena`. The hub and endless floors never build one.
  - Builders make no `ctx.rng` draws and never call `addEnemy`, `addProp`, `placeGem` or `addCheckpoint`. Enemies are built with `RunenpfadLevels.createEnemy`, so earlier zones keep their rng stream and enemy list. Gauntlets add no checkpoint.
  - Every hazard, platform, moving platform, pendulum, cannon, flame jet, enemy and crate carries `gauntlet: true`. Coins and hearts are not flagged. Each build appends `{variant, x0, x1, width}` to `stage.gauntlets` (`x0` and `x1` in px, `width` in tiles).
  - Rune chests (`placeChest`), the resonance path (`createResonancePath`), quest item spots (`questSpots`) and the moth/toad replacement skip gauntlet spans or flagged enemies. `variety.js` `safeGroundSpans` treats tiles over a gauntlet span or a flame jet's x-range as unsafe, so sky rays (and resonance paths) never hover there. The greedy and mend trial loops in `levels.js` skip extra enemies and bonus hearts whose tile lies inside a gauntlet (the rng draws stay the same) and clamp extra-enemy patrols at the span edges.
  - A spike trap with a custom `period` must also set `offset`. `sim.js` assigns a default offset only when `period` is absent.
  - Moving platforms are drawn 2 tiles wide, so their `w` is 96.
  - Rune trials cycle by level id (`variety.js` `pickMod`). No trapdoor variant (`crumble_steps`, `pendulum_bridge`, `crumble_chain`) may sit in a `fragile` trial level, because that trial scales trapdoor `crumbleTime`. No moving-platform variant (`ferry`) may sit in a `storm` trial level, because that trial multiplies `platformSpeed`.
  - Stage `parTime` is the config par plus `parBonusFor(zones)`, which adds 4 to 10 s per variant. `CAMPAIGN_LEVELS[i].parTime` stays unchanged.
  - Every variant must stay beatable for the base knight (no air jump, `maxAirJumps` 0). Each timed gate must keep a safe start window of at least 21 ticks (0.35 s), with at most 85 % of start times safe. `test-runenpfad-gauntlets.mjs` proves this with scripted solutions for both heroes and checks that naive runs fail.
  - **Checkpoint before the gauntlet** (`gauntlets.js` `checkpointBefore`, called by `levels.js` right before each gauntlet zone is built): campaign levels only, so the hub and endless floors get none, and no `rng` draw. If a checkpoint already lies within 8 tiles before the gauntlet start, nothing is added. Otherwise the 6 tiles left of the start are scanned for a flat solid tile with no solid tile above it, no platform in the three rows above it and no hazard in the two rows above it, outside every camp, place, thorn roller, flame jet and gate span. The nearest one gets a checkpoint (`{ id: 'cp_' + n, x, y, activated: false }`). If none qualifies, the function lays 2 flat ground tiles at the cursor, puts the checkpoint on the first of them and advances the cursor by 2, so the gauntlet starts 2 tiles later. The builders and `WIDTHS` are unchanged, and the gauntlet itself still adds no checkpoint. Activating any checkpoint sets `sim.checkpointUsed = false`; when a revive had been used, the notice is `runenpfad.notice.revive_ready`, otherwise `runenpfad.notice.checkpoint`.

## Known deviations and real behaviour

These are the places where the running code differs from the plans under `docs/superpowers/plans/` or where behaviour is easy to misread. The code is the contract.

- **Cutscene overlay drives itself.** It is not stepped through the app's `frame()`; `cutscene-ui.js` runs its own `requestAnimationFrame` loop while open.
- **Boot order.** On boot the sim is created first and the prologue plays over the paused stage.
- **Two scenes at once.** When the prologue and a world intro are both unseen (a start at level 6, 11, 16 or 21), both play, prologue first.
- **Prologue trigger.** `pending` returns the prologue for any campaign level 1–25 while it is unseen, for existing players too.
- **Chronicle seen state is computed.** `chronicle` reports a scene as seen when the unlocked level is beyond its trigger level, and it stores nothing; `profile.seen` only grows through `markSeen`.
- **Skipping marks the whole play as seen.** `finish` passes every id of the play to `onDone`, and `app.js` `markSeen` stores all of them, also when the player pressed Skip. A skipped story therefore counts as played in full.
- **Scene triggers live in `launchStage`, not `startStage`.** Boot, the world map and the win-card Next use `launchStage` and play the stage-start scenes. `startStage` (also exposed for tests) plays nothing. The won trigger runs in the tick when the stage is won.
- **Up/W does not dash inside an interior.** Only Space or the touch jump button does (`app.js` filters `jumpPressed` inside an interior).
- **Entering a door.** A door is only entered from the ground: `door.near` also needs the hero within 8 px of the ground line.
- **Endless caves rotate.** The endless generator picks its cave from a fixed rotation (`beast_den`, `crystal_grotto`, `sun_tomb`, `ice_cave`, `boiler_room`) by `floor(idx / 5) % 5`, not by biome.
- **Leaving a place writes nothing.** `Places.leave` only moves the hero and sets the timer. Room state is written as it changes in `placeState`.
- **Interior step order.** Iris timer, floats, a pending leave (returns early), hero, mobs, shots, things, the clear check, pickups, interaction, room exits, KO. A room change happens when the iris closes.
- **Village zone length.** The village zone is 65 tiles (the plan says about 64).
- **Cutscene view options.** `RunenpfadCutsceneView.create(canvas, {t})` takes the translator as an option.
- **Interior loot for endless.** Endless and repeat chests never write `profile.loot`; they pay 15 coins per open and are remembered only per stage run.
- **Validator extras.** `validate()` also checks that NPC and chest cells touch a reachable cell, that mobs start at least 4 cells from an entry, and that a `lit` prop condition is valid; these are not in the plan.
- **Hub has no key or portal.** `levels.js` skips the key and portal fallback placement for the hub, and the village zone places no gems.
- **Small changes done.** The hub camera hides the stage border at both street ends; the interior lock hint uses the key `runenpfad.notice.inside_locked`; the ambience bed is silenced while a cutscene plays.
- **Round 9 plan differences.** The telegraph ring fills its area in both branches, and the iris is drawn by `feel-fx.js` itself rather than by `interior-view.js` `drawWipe`. `TRIM` clamps to -14 to +24 dB (the plan said +16). Orbs and cannonballs are not turned, and crests are only flipped.
- **Guardian payloads.** `in_boss_tell` and `in_boss_attack` carry `pattern`; `in_boss_phase` and `in_boss_die` carry only `kind`.
- **Guardian tell length.** `MIN_TELL` is 0.65 s, above the 0.6 s the plan names; the bosses test checks 0.6 s.
- **Card keys.** The plan moved `cardKeys` from `app.js` to `hud.js`; the function is still in `app.js`.
- **Small keys unused.** Pots with `drop: 'key'`, `pickup: 'key'` and gates with a `key` part are implemented, but no room data uses them.
- **Recruit meetings.** `recruit_f_home` and `recruit_m_home` have no `MEETINGS` entry and no graph; the validator does not check meeting ids.
- **Hurt bump in music.** `music.js` `intensityFor` reads `hero.hurtTimer` for the interior danger bump, but interior heroes carry `hurtT`, so the bump never applies inside a room. Its nearest-mob loop tests `m.dead`, while interior mobs use the state `'dead'`.
- **Bounty timing.** `track` runs once per simulation call, after the substeps, so `portal_win` checks `sim.damageTaken` at the end of that call and not at the moment of the win.
