# Runenpfad — Virtual Desktop 2D Action-Platformer

## Purpose

Offline 2D action-platformer game (`runenpfad`, category `games`) for the AuraGo Virtual Desktop with a 25-level campaign across five biomes, multi-phase world bosses with telegraphed attack patterns, two playable heroes, persistent Rune Workshop upgrades, per-level Rune Trials, an Endless Rune Tower mode, parallax biome backdrops, adaptive procedural music and an optional WebGL2 post-processing stage.

Round 3 adds five enemy kinds (`spitter`, `swooper`, `shielded`, `phaser`, `brute`), stomping, four rune power-ups (`fire`, `frost`, `storm`, `magnet`), three new zones (`geyser_field`, `rune_gate`, `flame_jets`), a Codex-generated art atlas with sprite FX and large boss art, and adaptive camera zoom.

Round 4 adds swinging ropes (`swing_gorge`), three puzzle types (`rune_sequence` memory at a seer stone, `lever_bridge`, timed `ritual_chain`), dragon strafe set pieces with stone shelters (`dragon_strafe`), `drakeling` enemies and ember/frost dragon bosses in the endless tower.

## Ownership

- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/levels.js` — Biome, hero, enemy and upgrade tables, sprite manifest validator (`validateSpriteManifests`), the 25-level campaign stages and the seeded endless generator. Round 4: zone modules are dispatched through `zoneModules()`; the new stage arrays (`swings`, `oracles`, `glyphStones`, `levers`, `bridgeSegs`, `rituals`, `dragonRuns`) always exist.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/sim.js` — Pure `60 Hz` (`1/60 s`) simulation loop and stage state; delegates to `physics`, `combat`, `bosses`, `zones`, `zones-extra`, `runes`, `swing`, `puzzles` and `dragons`; calls `RunenpfadSwing.update` every tick. Also owns collectibles, checkpoints, star scoring and the persistence re-export.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/physics.js` — Tile collision, the event queue (`pushEvent`), fx wrappers and the seeded gameplay RNG (`Physics.rand` over `sim.rngState`).
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/combat.js` — Player and enemy damage, hurt and invulnerability, stomps, crates, hero attacks, projectiles, the kill combo, rune shield and frost absorb, and the timed temporary hazards.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/bosses.js` — World boss AI: intro, telegraphed attack state machine, per-pattern telegraphs and the phase 2 escalation. Round 4 adds dragon boss patterns `strafe` (temp hazard `dragonfire`), `fireballs`, `roar` (summons up to 2 drakelings) and `dive`; dragon bosses scale 3.2 and hover at `spawnY - 130`. Endless boss floors from 6 on alternate between the world boss and the dragon (frost in glacier, ember elsewhere).
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/zones.js` — Variety zone builders: `bounce_grove`, `updraft_shaft`, `conveyor_works`, `pendulum_hall`, `secret_vault`, `lantern_path`, `rope_chasm`, plus their per-tick steps, forces and interactions.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/zones-extra.js` — Round 3 zone builders and steps: `geyser_field` (`stage.geysers`), `rune_gate` (`stage.switches`, `stage.gates`) and `flame_jets` (`stage.flameJets`). Global `RunenpfadZonesExtra`.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/swing.js` — DOM-free rope swinging (global `RunenpfadSwing`): `swing_gorge` builder, pendulum physics, grab, pump and release. `stage.swings[] {id, group, style 'vine'|'rope'|'chain', ax, ay, len, angle, omega, phase}`, `player.swing {id, angle, omega, grip, t}` and `player.animState = 'swing'`.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/puzzles.js` — DOM-free puzzles (global `RunenpfadPuzzles`): `rune_sequence` (`stage.oracles`, `stage.glyphStones`, vault gate), `lever_bridge` (`stage.levers`, `stage.bridgeSegs`; segments are ordinary platform tiles with `puzzle` set and `solid` toggled) and `ritual_chain` (`stage.rituals`: plate, stairs, braziers, 10 s `timeLimit`). Zone variants use a `:vault` / `:req` suffix in campaign zone slots.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/dragons.js` — DOM-free `dragon_strafe` set piece (global `RunenpfadDragons`): `stage.dragonRuns` state machine far → warn → strafe → leave, and the shelter rule `sheltered(sim)`.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/runes.js` — Rune power-ups `fire`, `frost`, `storm` and `magnet`: `player.rune = {kind,timeLeft,duration,charges}`, rune orbs (`{type:'rune_orb', kind}` collectibles), rune chests (`stage.chests`), chest placement, frost absorb, storm zap and magnet pull. Global `RunenpfadRunes`.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/behaviors.js` — Enemy behaviour profiles (`patrol`, `charger`, `jumper`, `flyer`, `turret`, `ambusher`, `bomber`, `spitter`, `swooper`, `shielded`, `phaser`, `brute`, `drakeling`) driven by the tile helpers the simulation passes in. `drakeling` hovers 180–260 px away and spits an aimed `fireball` about every 2.4 s.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/variety.js` — Rune Trials (per-level modifiers) and the shrine blessing state.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/profile.js` — Save schema (`aurago.runenpfad.v1`), clamping, `music` and `gfx` preferences, workshop costs and hero stats.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/fx.js` — Deterministic particle and screen-effect system (particles, shake, flash, impact freeze). Owns its own visual RNG.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/biomes.js` — Per-biome presentation data: sky, parallax layer specs, light, weather, colour grade and ambience beds.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/audio.js` — Gesture-unlocked Web Audio controller: cue table, sample and synth layers, buses, per-biome reverb, master limiter and `validateSoundManifest` for `aurago-sounds`. Round 4 adds dragon, rope and puzzle cues.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/sfx.js` — DOM-free synth recipe bank (global `RunenpfadSfx`) used by `audio.js`. Round 4 adds the dragon, rope and puzzle recipes, including the four glyph tones C, E, G and A. Loaded right before `audio.js`.

- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/backdrops.js` — Backdrop painters, part 1: shared noise helpers, woodland, cavern and cave-ceiling silhouettes.

- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/view.js` — Canvas 2D renderer that composes the layers above, reads simulation state and feeds the post-processing stage.
- `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/hud.js` — Rune-stone HUD builder (`RunenpfadHud`): top bar with sprite icons, notice/tag row, boss bar and boss card, rune plaque, key-cap button helper, win-card stars and plaques; builds DOM through the `el` helper that `app.js` passes in. - `ui/js/desktop/apps/runenpfad/app.js` — Window shell (`RunenpfadApp`), dialogs and round cards, keyboard (round cards: Enter/Space/J primary, R replay, B/S workshop, Esc worlds, 350 ms guard, no auto-repeat), touch and pointer input, resize and visibility handling, and disposal.
- `ui/img/runenpfad/*.webp`, `ui/img/runenpfad/atlas.json`, `ui/img/runenpfad/provenance.json` — 37 original sprite sheets generated with Codex CLI image generation (bosses, new enemies, effects, portal, rune-stone checkpoint, chest, crystal switch, rune orbs, and round 4 dragons, drakeling, fireball, dragon fire, puzzle props and rope props). ## Local Contracts

- **Game Maker asset reuse**: Reads `/api/game-maker/asset-packs/<pack>/...` for `jump-and-run`, `human-characters-animated`, `monsters-animals-animated`, `robots-drones-animated`, `assets-animated`, `nature-assets`, and `aurago-sounds`. Never copy PNGs, WAVs, or manifests into `ui/`. Runenpfad's OWN generated art lives in `ui/img/runenpfad/` (like Galaxa in `ui/img/galaxa/`).
- **Optional art atlas**: `atlas.json` is optional. When it fails to load, the game renders exactly as before with pack sprites. Each atlas-drawn enemy def carries `art` plus a pack `prefix` fallback. Never hand-edit `atlas.json`, `provenance.json` or the WebP sheets. Regenerate with `py -3.13 scripts/build_runenpfad_art.py --import <raw> <prompts> [ids…]` (imports only the named new sheets; existing WebPs stay byte-identical), then `--write`; `--check` must pass. The atlas holds 37 sheets (5.45 MB). The build script supports a `left` pivot (dragon fire stream) and a `dragon` clip kind (idle, telegraph, attack, roar, enraged, hurt, defeat). `drakeling` falls back to pack prefix `quadcopter_`.
- **DOM-free core**: `levels.js`, `sim.js`, `profile.js`, `behaviors.js`, `variety.js`, `fx.js`, `biomes.js`, `audio.js`, `sfx.js`, `physics.js`, `combat.js`, `bosses.js`, `zones.js`, `zones-extra.js`, `runes.js`, `swing.js`, `puzzles.js`, `dragons.js` and `music.js` carry zero DOM references so `scripts/test-runenpfad-sim.mjs` and `scripts/test-runenpfad-audio.mjs` execute them directly under Node `vm`. `scripts/test-runenpfad-sim.mjs` must load `swing.js`, `puzzles.js` and `dragons.js` itself. Do not add `document`, `window` or canvas use to them.
- **Canvas-facing modules**: `backdrops.js`, `backdrops-biomes.js`, `atmosphere.js`, `decor.js`, `art.js`, `actors.js`, `stage-art.js`, `set-pieces.js`, `postfx.js` and `view.js`. Only `app.js` and `hud.js` build DOM.
- **Deterministic gameplay randomness**: No `Math.random` and no `Date.now` in the DOM-free gameplay and audio modules. Gameplay decisions use only `Physics.rand` on `sim.rngState`. Never use `fx.rng` for gameplay.
- **Read-only renderer**: The canvas-facing modules never mutate simulation state. Visual effects are driven by events and read-only state.
- **Event vocabulary**: Simulation events are emitted through `Physics.pushEvent`. Besides the existing set, the earlier new events are `pickup_star`, `pickup_treasure`, `hazard`, `door_open`, `enemy_attack`, `spike_rise`, `player_fall`, `boss_intro`, `boss_telegraph`, `boss_attack`, `boss_slam`, `combo`, `bounce`, `lantern_lit` and `secret_break`. Round 3 adds `enemy_block`, `enemy_phase {phased}`, `enemy_windup`, `enemy_slam`, `spore_burst`, `stomp {enemyId}`, `chest_open {kind}`, `rune_pickup {kind}`, `rune_expire {kind}`, `rune_shield_break`, `rune_zap {x1,y1,x2,y2}`, `geyser_burst`, `switch_on`, `gate_open`, `jet_warn` and `jet_fire`. Round 4 adds `swing_grab`, `swing_release {speed}`, `seq_show {oracleId, glyph, index}`, `seq_hit {glyph, step}`, `seq_fail`, `seq_solved`, `lever_pull {on}`, `bridge_toggle {on}`, `bridge_done`, `plate_down`, `stairs_up`, `stairs_down`, `brazier_lit {order}`, `ritual_done`, `ritual_fail`, `dragon_roar {kind}`, `dragon_warn {x1, x2, laneY, laneH, kind, dir}`, `dragon_breath {kind}`, `dragon_pass {kind}` and `fireball_hit`. Puzzle and dragon events carry world `x`/`y` where meaningful.
- **Enemy event payloads**: Enemy-originated events carry `defId` plus `x`/`y`. Audio identifies enemies by `defId` and pans by `x`. `enemy_attack.kind` is `shot`, `lunge`, `dive` (crystal bat), `lob` (sporecap) or `fireball` (drakeling and dragon boss). Hazard kinds add `poison`, `flame_jet` and `dragonfire`. `boss_intro`, `boss_phase` and `boss_defeat` carry `defId`.
- **Stomp**: Falling onto an enemy's top (`vy > 120`, not phased) deals 1 damage (2 with the fire rune) and bounces the hero. The bounce is higher while jump is held (`app.js` `readInput` reports held `jump`). Bosses take at most one stomp per 0.6 s.
- **Shielded and phased enemies**: A shielded scarab blocks hits from the side it faces. Rear hits, stomps and the knight's air slam pierce. Phased frost wraiths are intangible.
- **Camera**: Adaptive zoom `clamp(viewH/576, 1, 2)` (about 12 tiles), multiplied by 0.86 while a boss is alive, clamped to `[0.85, 2]`. World layers draw under the zoomed transform. Sky, parallax and weather draw in screen space. The camera never shows below the stage bottom. Positions handed to postfx and lighting are converted to screen space. `getCamera()` and the frame view expose world extents, which audio panning depends on.
- **Module load order**: `module-loader.js` must list `biomes` → `fx` → `physics` → `behaviors` → `bosses` → `variety` → `profile` → `runes` → `zones` → `zones-extra` → `swing` → `puzzles` → `dragons` → `levels` → `combat` → `sim` → `sfx` → `audio` → `music` → `backdrops` → `backdrops-biomes` → `atmosphere` → `decor` → `art` → `set-pieces` → `actors` → `stage-art` → `postfx` → `view` → `hud` → `app`. Each module reads its dependencies from the shared global scope at call time, so a new module must be placed after everything it reads.
- **Music**: Each boss theme has its own lead instrument: boar horn with war drums (boar), goblin dark bells with plucked strings (goblin), desert orb low reed over a drone (desert), glacier glass bells (glacier), and a citadel half-time machine groove with fuzz bass (citadel). Boss themes stay minor or phrygian. The sim test forbids a major third in `boss_desert`.
- **Pause dialog order**: `resume`, `restart`, `sound`, `music`, `gfx`, `reset`. The browser test clicks the first `aria-pressed` button, so keep `resume` first and keep the toggles in this order. `gfx` is hidden while no GPU stage is attached and the preference is `auto`.
- **Save integrity**: `importProfile` validates `v === 1` and clamps `unlockedLevel`, `endlessBestFloor`, `coins`, `totalScore`, `upgrades`, `starsByLevel`, `bestTimeByLevel`, and the `music` (boolean, default on) and `gfx` (`auto` or `canvas`) preferences. Invalid saves fall back cleanly to defaults. The save format stays `aurago.runenpfad.v1`.
- **Climb clips**: Ladder and rope climbing use `<hero>_climb_ladder` and `<hero>_climb_rope`. These are rear-view clips and are never flipped. The clock runs only while moving, reverses when descending, and falls back to the `<hero>_idle` clip when a clip is missing.
- **Swing state**: While hanging, `player.swing` is set and the sim sets `player.animState = 'swing'`; the hands sit at the rope end. The renderer reads that state without changing it.
- **Puzzle solvability**: Puzzles must stay solvable for both heroes with unlimited retries. Oracles replay after a hit, every lever preset is checked by the sim test (toggles are self-inverse), ritual retries go through the plate, and a hero can always drop down when stairs sink. Required puzzle gates are full-height `portcullis` gates.
- **Dragon fire is avoidable**: Every strafe has a warn phase of at least 1.2 s and only one strafe runs at a time. A solid tile within 3 tiles above the hero (`SHELTER_TILES` in `dragons.js`) shelters from fire; fire hurts only an exposed hero. The set piece emits a `hazard` event with `kind: 'dragonfire'` and a `frost` flag; the boss `dragonfire` temp hazard carries `variant` ('ember'|'frost') and `dir`.
- **No third-party runtime**: PixiJS and Phaser are deliberately not used. The GPU stage is a full-screen post-process, and raw WebGL2 avoids an 800 KB vendor bundle and a CSP `unsafe-eval` shim. Do not add a vendor game library.
- **Post-processing never breaks the game**: `postfx` must never throw and never leave a rejected promise. Every failure calls `onFallback` once and falls back to the 2D canvas. `app.js` then sets `data-rp-gfx` (`gpu` or `canvas`) on the shell, and `data-rp-gfx-reason` records the fallback reason. The 2D `.rp-canvas` always stays in the DOM because the browser test reads its pixels.
- **Safe UI & styling**: `app.js` and `hud.js` build DOM via `document.createElement` and `textContent` only (no `.innerHTML`, no external URLs). `desktop-app-runenpfad.css` is listed in `desktopAppMaterialsExempt` (`ui/desktop_app_materials_test.go`), scopes classes to `.rp-*` (never `cc-` or `ad-`), and avoids `!important` and CSS `animation:`.
- **Registration & bundles**: Registered in `internal/desktop/types.go` (`games`, `open_maximized: "true"`, `logo_path: "/img/runenpfad/icon.svg"`), `desktop-foundation.js`, `module-loader.js`, `menus-and-routing.js`, and `window-shell-runtime.js`; bundled into `ui/js/desktop/bundles/main.bundle.js`.

## Work Guidance

- Keep each first-party JS file under `ui/js/desktop/` below `1100` lines with semantic filenames (`TestVirtualDesktopFirstPartyJSFilesStayBelowLineBudget`, `TestVirtualDesktopJSUsesSemanticChunkNames`). Split new subsystems into their own module rather than growing `sim.js` or `levels.js`. Round 4 files near the budget: `actors.js` (~984), `set-pieces.js` (~988), `levels.js` (~967) and `music.js` (~1065).
- Sound (`profile.sound`) and music (`profile.music`) are enabled by default. Sound toggles from the top HUD bar or the pause menu. Music toggles from the pause menu. Audio unlocks on the first user gesture. Suspend audio whenever the window becomes inactive, minimized, hidden, or a modal is open.
- Graphics (`profile.gfx`) defaults to `auto`, which uses the GPU stage when available. The pause menu switches it to `canvas`.
- The impact freeze (`fx.hitstop`) freezes the world but must keep resolving player input (attack, heal) and pickups, otherwise gameplay and the existing simulation tests break.
- New art comes from Codex CLI image generation as transparent RGBA sheets: regular grids, characters facing right, grounded ones on a common baseline. The raw sheets contain near-invisible low-alpha fog, so the build script zeroes alpha below 24 and measures frames at alpha 48 or higher. Keep the atlas optional and guard every new visual when its stage array is absent (`stage.geysers`, `stage.switches`, `stage.gates`, `stage.flameJets`, `stage.chests`, `stage.swings`, `stage.oracles`, `stage.glyphStones`, `stage.levers`, `stage.bridgeSegs`, `stage.rituals`, `stage.dragonRuns`).
