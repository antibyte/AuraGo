# Runenpfad — Virtual Desktop 2D Action-Platformer

## Purpose

Offline 2D action-platformer game (`runenpfad`, category `games`) for the AuraGo Virtual Desktop with a 25-level campaign across five biomes, multi-phase world bosses with telegraphed attack patterns, two playable heroes, persistent Rune Workshop upgrades, per-level Rune Trials, an Endless Rune Tower mode, parallax biome backdrops, adaptive procedural music and an optional WebGL2 post-processing stage.

Round 3 adds five enemy kinds (`spitter`, `swooper`, `shielded`, `phaser`, `brute`), stomping, four rune power-ups (`fire`, `frost`, `storm`, `magnet`), three new zones (`geyser_field`, `rune_gate`, `flame_jets`), a Codex-generated art atlas with sprite FX and large boss art, and adaptive camera zoom.

Round 4 adds swinging ropes (`swing_gorge`), three puzzle types (`rune_sequence` memory at a seer stone, `lever_bridge`, timed `ritual_chain`), dragon strafe set pieces with stone shelters (`dragon_strafe`), `drakeling` enemies and ember/frost dragon bosses in the endless tower.

NPC encounters add six recurring characters (`witch`, `wizard`, `merchant`, `chief`, `seer`, `alchemist`) who stand at camps in campaign levels and endless floors. The hero talks to them through offline scripted choice dialogues (16 locales), trades with them, and builds reputation, remembered flags and quests that persist in the profile. A character can also be fought in a real-time close-up duel. Each world carries a 2–3 meeting arc, and level 25 holds the finale.

## Local Contracts

- **Game Maker asset reuse**: Reads `/api/game-maker/asset-packs/<pack>/...` for `jump-and-run`, `human-characters-animated`, `monsters-animals-animated`, `robots-drones-animated`, `assets-animated`, `nature-assets`, and `aurago-sounds`. Never copy PNGs, WAVs, or manifests into `ui/`. Runenpfad's OWN generated art lives in `ui/img/runenpfad/` (like Galaxa in `ui/img/galaxa/`).
- **Optional art atlas**: `atlas.json` is optional. When it fails to load, the game renders exactly as before with pack sprites. Each atlas-drawn enemy def carries `art` plus a pack `prefix` fallback. Never hand-edit `atlas.json`, `provenance.json` or the WebP sheets. Regenerate with `py -3.13 scripts/build_runenpfad_art.py --import <raw> <prompts> [ids…]` (imports only the named new sheets; existing WebPs stay byte-identical), then `--write`; `--check` must pass. The atlas holds 77 sheets (about 13.2 MB). The build script supports a `left` pivot (dragon fire stream) and a `dragon` clip kind (idle, telegraph, attack, roar, enraged, hurt, defeat). `drakeling` falls back to pack prefix `quadcopter_`.
- **NPC and duel art**: `npc-<id>` (level sprites, 4×2, feet pivot, eager), `ui-npc-icons` (emote icons, 4×2, eager), `portrait-<id>` (dialogue busts, 3×1, lazy, alpha cut 110), `duel-<id>` and `duel-knight` / `duel-ranger` (close-up duel sheets, 4×4, lazy) and `fx-duel-spells` (duel FX, lazy). The build uses the kinds `npc`, `portrait`, `duel`, `ui` and `fx`; NPC sheets use alpha cut 40. Large portrait and duel sprites draw with `opts.pixel` (no smoothing).
- **DOM-free core**: `levels.js`, `sim.js`, `profile.js`, `behaviors.js`, `variety.js`, `fx.js`, `biomes.js`, `audio.js`, `sfx.js`, `physics.js`, `combat.js`, `bosses.js`, `zones.js`, `zones-extra.js`, `runes.js`, `swing.js`, `puzzles.js`, `dragons.js`, `music.js`, `npcs.js`, `npc-story.js`, `npc-story-late.js`, `dialogue.js`, `trade.js`, `duel-patterns.js`, `duel.js` and `duel-music.js` carry zero DOM references so `scripts/test-runenpfad-sim.mjs`, `scripts/test-runenpfad-npc.mjs` and `scripts/test-runenpfad-audio.mjs` execute them directly under Node `vm`. `scripts/test-runenpfad-sim.mjs` must load `swing.js`, `puzzles.js`, `dragons.js` and the camp modules (`npcs.js`, `npc-story.js`, `npc-story-late.js`, `dialogue.js`, `trade.js`) itself. Do not add `document`, `window` or canvas use to them.
- **Canvas-facing modules**: `backdrops.js`, `backdrops-biomes.js`, `atmosphere.js`, `decor.js`, `art.js`, `actors.js`, `stage-art.js`, `set-pieces.js`, `npc-view.js`, `duel-view.js`, `postfx.js` and `view.js`. Only `app.js`, `hud.js` and `dialogue-ui.js` build DOM.

Never use `fx.rng` for gameplay. The duel's visual particle system is seeded from `sim.rngState` without advancing it.
- **Read-only renderer**: The canvas-facing modules never mutate simulation state. Visual effects are driven by events and read-only state.

Besides the existing set, the earlier new events are `pickup_star`, `pickup_treasure`, `hazard`, `door_open`, `enemy_attack`, `spike_rise`, `player_fall`, `boss_intro`, `boss_telegraph`, `boss_attack`, `boss_slam`, `combo`, `bounce`, `lantern_lit` and `secret_break`. Round 3 adds `enemy_block`, `enemy_phase {phased}`, `enemy_windup`, `enemy_slam`, `spore_burst`, `stomp {enemyId}`, `chest_open {kind}`, `rune_pickup {kind}`, `rune_expire {kind}`, `rune_shield_break`, `rune_zap {x1,y1,x2,y2}`, `geyser_burst`, `switch_on`, `gate_open`, `jet_warn` and `jet_fire`. Puzzle and dragon events carry world `x`/`y` where meaningful. NPC: `npc_near`, `npc_talk`, `npc_choice {node}`, `npc_rep {faction, delta, dir}`, `npc_trade {npcId, offerId, fake}`, `npc_quest {questId, state}`, `quest_pickup {kind, x, y, count}`, `npc_provoke`, `duel_start`, `duel_tell {pattern}`, `duel_attack {pattern}`, `duel_hit {target, dmg}`, `duel_block {by}`, `duel_roll`, `duel_phase`, `duel_special {kind}` and `duel_end {result}`.
- **Enemy event payloads**: Enemy-originated events carry `defId` plus `x`/`y`. Audio identifies enemies by `defId` and pans by `x`. `enemy_attack.kind` is `shot`, `lunge`, `dive` (crystal bat), `lob` (sporecap) or `fireball` (drakeling and dragon boss). Hazard kinds add `poison`, `flame_jet` and `dragonfire`. `boss_intro`, `boss_phase` and `boss_defeat` carry `defId`. Duel events carry no `x`: the duel is its own arena, and audio would pan it by the world camera.
- **Stomp**: Falling onto an enemy's top (`vy > 120`, not phased) deals 1 damage (2 with the fire rune) and bounces the hero. The bounce is higher while jump is held (`app.js` `readInput` reports held `jump`). Bosses take at most one stomp per 0.6 s.
- **Shielded and phased enemies**: A shielded scarab blocks hits from the side it faces. Rear hits, stomps and the knight's air slam pierce. Phased frost wraiths are intangible.
- **Camera**: Adaptive zoom `clamp(viewH/576, 1, 2)` (about 12 tiles), multiplied by 0.86 while a boss is alive, clamped to `[0.85, 2]`. World layers draw under the zoomed transform. Sky, parallax and weather draw in screen space. The camera never shows below the stage bottom. Positions handed to postfx and lighting are converted to screen space. `getCamera()` and the frame view expose world extents, which audio panning depends on.
- **Module load order**: `module-loader.js` must list `biomes` → `fx` → `physics` → `behaviors` → `bosses` → `variety` → `profile` → `runes` → `zones` → `zones-extra` → `swing` → `puzzles` → `dragons` → `npcs` → `npc-story` → `npc-story-late` → `dialogue` → `trade` → `duel-patterns` → `duel` → `levels` → `combat` → `sim` → `sfx` → `audio` → `music` → `duel-music` → `backdrops` → `backdrops-biomes` → `parallax-art` → `atmosphere` → `decor` → `art` → `set-pieces` → `actors` → `stage-art` → `npc-view` → `duel-view` → `postfx` → `view` → `hud` → `dialogue-ui` → `app`. Each module reads its dependencies from the shared global scope at call time, so a new module must be placed after everything it reads.
- **Painted parallax**: `biomes.js` carries an optional `art` block per biome (`sheets`, `far`, `mid`, `near`, cavern `ceiling`: factor, lift, haze, dark, scale, gap). `art.js` keeps `bg-*` sheets out of the eager boot load (`atlas.lazy`); `RunenpfadArt.loadSheet(atlas, id)` loads one on demand and never rejects, and `RunenpfadArt.current()` returns the decoded atlas. `parallax-art.js` requests only the current biome sheets on the first painted frame; until they decode (or if they fail) the procedural silhouette layers draw, then the painted layers fade in over 0.7 s and the silhouettes step aside (sky, sun, clouds, aurora, shafts, stars and weather stay procedural). Far bands sit behind a mid hill body, near props stand on the ground line, nothing behind the gameplay layer may read as a platform, and no painted layer draws in the bottom 40 px beyond the existing body fill (the browser test scans that row).
- **Music**: Each boss theme has its own lead instrument: boar horn with war drums (boar), goblin dark bells with plucked strings (goblin), desert orb low reed over a drone (desert), glacier glass bells (glacier), and a citadel half-time machine groove with fuzz bass (citadel). Boss themes stay minor or phrygian. The sim test forbids a major third in `boss_desert`. Duels play one loop per character (`duel_<npc>`, selected by `RunenpfadDuelMusic.themeFor` while `sim.encounter` is a duel); each loop is boss-style and has no major third.
- **Pause dialog order**: `resume`, `restart`, `sound`, `music`, `gfx`, `reset`. The browser test clicks the first `aria-pressed` button, so keep `resume` first and keep the toggles in this order. `gfx` is hidden while no GPU stage is attached and the preference is `auto`.
- **Save integrity**: `importProfile` validates `v === 1` and clamps `unlockedLevel`, `endlessBestFloor`, `coins`, `totalScore`, `upgrades`, `starsByLevel`, `bestTimeByLevel`, and the `music` (boolean, default on) and `gfx` (`auto` or `canvas`) preferences. Invalid saves fall back cleanly to defaults. The save format stays `aurago.runenpfad.v1`. In `app.js`, `rejectedRaw` is declared before the first `readProfile()` call: `readProfile` assigns it, so a later `let` throws inside the `try` and every saved profile is replaced by the defaults. `TestRunenpfadRegistration` checks the order, and the browser test reopens the app and expects the unlocked stage and the bought upgrade.
- **Story save caps**: `importProfile` clamps the story fields. `rep[faction]` is −5..5 (unknown factions dropped). `npcFlags` keys must match `FLAG_RE` (`/^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,15}\.[a-z0-9_]{1,23}$/`), values are 0..99, zeros are dropped, and the first 64 sorted keys are kept. `inventory` accepts only `STORY_ITEMS` ids (caps: `glow_mushroom`, `sun_shard`, `gear_part` 9; `hex_charm` 1; `lucky_amulet`, `discount_token` 3; `rune_attune` 2). `quests.active` (max 8) and `quests.done` (max 16) take only `QUEST_IDS`, deduplicated, with `done` winning over `active`. A missing or invalid block falls back to its default. `app.js` ignores raw saves over 16384 characters, so new collections must stay capped.
- **Encounter state**: `sim.encounter = null | {kind: 'dialogue'|'duel', npcRef, npcId, nodeId, panel: 'talk'|'trade', returnNode, seq, openedTick, duel?}` exists only at runtime. `seq` changes on every node or panel change and is the dialogue window's re-render key. `sim.mode` stays `'campaign'|'endless'` (stage restart, win card and HUD depend on it); never reuse it for an encounter. `sim.state` stays `'playing'` during an encounter, so `isPaused()` and the music state logic are unchanged.
- **Encounter hold**: `stepOneTick` calls `RunenpfadNpcs.hold(sim, input, dt)` before the world advances. While a dialogue is open it returns true, so the clock, runes, enemies and hazards stay frozen. During a duel the hook steps `RunenpfadDuel` instead of the level and calls `finishDuel` when `done`. Boss intros and slow motion do not pause the world, so do not reuse them.
- **Talk input**: `talkPressed` is `'e'` (E or the touch button) or `'up'` (Up or W). Up opens a dialogue only when the hero is on the ground, off ladders and ropes, with `|vx| < 60`, and only a dialogue that actually opens swallows the jump. In a duel, any `talkPressed` other than `'up'` is the rune special. `sim.js` clears the edge on every substep, so one press cannot fire twice.
- **Duel lock and no escape**: While `sim.encounter.kind === 'duel'`, restart (the `R` key and the pause button), the level select, hero switching and progress reset are disabled, and `R` is ignored during any encounter. The walls clamp the hero, and `Esc` only pauses; resuming continues the duel. The duel ends only with a win or a loss.
- **Story flushing**: Every story mutation sets `sim.storyDirty`. `app.js` adopts it on the next tick (`adoptSimProfile` also writes the save). `flushStory()` runs at stage start, before the level, shop and pause dialogs open, on workshop purchases, at cleanup, and from the dialogue window after replies and purchases. Stage changes and menus must never see a stale saved profile.
- **Dialogue graphs**: `npc-story*.js` data is interpreted by `dialogue.js`. A node has 1–4 choices, at least one without `if`. Every node is reachable from a meeting start or hook target, and every node can reach a closing choice (`to: null`, or a duel or provoke effect), so there are no dead ends. Every `to` exists. Effects use only the known op list, and a negative coin or item effect needs a `coinsMin` or `item` guard. `RunenpfadDialogue.validateGraphs()` returns the problems, and the npc test asserts an empty list. Meetings use first-match-wins starts, and the last start has no `if`.
- **Dialogue keys**: Node text is `runenpfad.npc.<npc>.<node>`; choice text is `…<node>.c<n>` unless the choice names a shared key (`runenpfad.npc.c.farewell|thanks|trade|leave|fight|back`). Tip text is `runenpfad.npc.tip.<kind>`. Placeholders are `{tip}`, `{count}`, `{price}`, `{coins}` and `{name}`. Every locale keeps exactly the placeholders of `en`.
- **Tips**: Generated from live stage data: `key_dir` (key direction or "behind the boss"), `gems_left`, `secret_wall`, `chest_dir`, `puzzle_hint` (oracles, levers, rituals) and `boss_weakness` (per world boss, dragon frost included). Hidden choices are dropped before indexing, and `roll` uses `Physics.rand(sim)`.
- **Trade rules**: Price is `max(1, round(base × (1 − 0.06 × rep)))` for the seller's faction, so +5 gives 70 % and −5 gives 130 %. The merchant's haggle flag (`merchant.haggle`, cleared at stage start) takes a further 15 % off for the stage. Purchases spend `sim.profile.coins`, set `storyDirty`, and track stock per NPC per stage. Kobold fakes: the merchant at `kobolds ≤ −2` sells a fake with `Physics.rand` chance 0.35, which spends the coins and applies nothing. The reveal happens only when the dialogue closes (`RunenpfadTrade.onClose`), so an offer card never marks a fake. `map_charm` sets `sim.mapCharm` for the stage (reset at stage start); `npc-view.js` `drawCharm` then paints a glow ring on every uncollected gem on screen and an edge arrow toward each off-screen one (read-only, nothing in the bottom 40 px, steady under reduced motion). `rune_attune` is stored (cap 2); `runes.js` `grant` multiplies the next rune's duration by 1.5, consumes one through `addItem`, pushes `rune_attuned {x, y}` and the float `runenpfad.npc.float_attuned`.
- **Duel rules** (`duel.js`, `duel-patterns.js`): Deterministic: randomness only through `Physics.rand`. Hero stats come from `sim.stats` (`maxHp = 8 + 2·maxHp`, `hit = 2 + damage`). Duel damage never calls `hurtPlayer`. A loss sets `hp = max(1, hp − 1)`, `invulnTimer = 2`, `damageTaken += 1` and the `<npc>.duel_lost` flag, and never ends the run. A win sets `<npc>.beaten`, and its coins are paid only the first time. The special needs a rune active when the duel starts, and works once per duel. Potions and the heal key do nothing in a duel. Tells last at least 0.45 s in both phases, and each `duel_attack` follows its own `duel_tell`. Phase 2 starts once, at 50 % HP or below.
- **Provoke**: Attacking an NPC in front of them (`J`, `Z` or `Enter`, within 70 px) applies once per NPC per stage: the faction loses 1, the graph's `onAttack` effects run, `npc_provoke` is pushed, and the duel starts. Dialogue effects `provoke` and `duel` use the same path.
- **Camps**: The `npc_camp:<variant>` zone (`witch`, `merchant`, `chief`, `seer`, `wizard`, `alchemist`, two-NPC `a+b`, and `chief_gate`, which adds a portcullis column tied to the Krax gate) is 12 tiles of flat ground (13 with the gate). Camps place no enemies, no hazards and make no rng draws. `resolve()` runs once per stage (`stage.npcsReady`): it moves enemies spawned inside a camp outward, clamps patrol bounds, spawns quest items (`stage.questItems`), resets the per-stage trade flags and applies the citadel sabotage. Unknown zone names are skipped silently in `levels.js`, so camps disappear cleanly when `npcs.js` is not loaded.
- **Quests**: Quest items spawn on platforms in the quest's worlds (`witch_mushrooms` in worlds 0–1, `seer_shards` in world 2, `alchemist_gears` in world 4). Walking onto an item adds it to the profile inventory (capped) and marks it taken. `QUESTS` in `npcs.js` holds the item kind, count and floating label keys.
- **Hero switch**: Switching heroes restarts the stage. Once the running stage has score or more than 20 s on the clock, `chooseHero` arms the choice and needs a second click within 4 s (top bar button and workshop cards share this). On the win card the switch keeps the card and applies to the next stage. `startStage`, `openLevels` and `openPause` disarm it. Pick buttons are disabled during a duel.
- **Floating labels**: `sim.floatingTexts` entries are `{ x, y, text, color, timer, key?, suffix?, icon? }`. The DOM-free core never translates: `text` is the fallback, `key` a `runenpfad.*` translation key and `suffix` an untranslated tail such as ` 1/3`. The renderer draws `t(key) + suffix` when the key resolves, otherwise `text`, and paints `icon` (a `jump-and-run` asset id such as `heart`, `coin`, `potion`, `star`) behind the label. Every literal `key` in the core must exist in all 16 locales; the sim test scans `sim.js`, `combat.js`, `runes.js` and `zones-extra.js` for them. Literal keys in `npcs.js` and `trade.js` should follow the same rule; the sim test's scan list (`sim.js`, `combat.js`, `runes.js`, `zones-extra.js`) does not name them.
- **Win card mirrors the star rules**: The three goal rows of `hud.paintWinSummary` repeat the conditions of `finalizeVictory` in `sim.js` (portal reached, 3 gems, par time or no damage). Change both together. `sim.newBest` is true only when a stored best time was beaten; a first clear sets the time without the tag. On level 25 the win card also shows `sim.epilogue` (the epilogue plaque).
- **Climb clips**: Ladder and rope climbing use `<hero>_climb_ladder` and `<hero>_climb_rope`. These are rear-view clips and are never flipped. The clock runs only while moving, reverses when descending, and falls back to the `<hero>_idle` clip when a clip is missing.
- **Swing state**: While hanging, `player.swing` is set and the sim sets `player.animState = 'swing'`; the hands sit at the rope end. The renderer reads that state without changing it.
- **Puzzle solvability**: Puzzles must stay solvable for both heroes with unlimited retries. Oracles replay after a hit, every lever preset is checked by the sim test (toggles are self-inverse), ritual retries go through the plate, and a hero can always drop down when stairs sink. Required puzzle gates are full-height `portcullis` gates.
- **Dragon fire is avoidable**: Every strafe has a warn phase of at least 1.2 s and only one strafe runs at a time. A solid tile within 3 tiles above the hero (`SHELTER_TILES` in `dragons.js`) shelters from fire; fire hurts only an exposed hero. The set piece emits a `hazard` event with `kind: 'dragonfire'` and a `frost` flag; the boss `dragonfire` temp hazard carries `variant` ('ember'|'frost') and `dir`.
- **No third-party runtime**: PixiJS and Phaser are deliberately not used. The GPU stage is a full-screen post-process, and raw WebGL2 avoids an 800 KB vendor bundle and a CSP `unsafe-eval` shim. Do not add a vendor game library.
- **Post-processing never breaks the game**: `postfx` must never throw and never leave a rejected promise. Every failure calls `onFallback` once and falls back to the 2D canvas. `app.js` then sets `data-rp-gfx` (`gpu` or `canvas`) on the shell, and `data-rp-gfx-reason` records the fallback reason. The 2D `.rp-canvas` always stays in the DOM because the browser test reads its pixels.
- **Safe UI & styling**: `app.js`, `hud.js` and `dialogue-ui.js` build DOM via `document.createElement` and `textContent` only (no `.innerHTML`, no external URLs). `desktop-app-runenpfad.css` is listed in `desktopAppMaterialsExempt` (`ui/desktop_app_materials_test.go`), scopes classes to `.rp-*` (never `cc-` or `ad-`), and avoids `!important` and CSS `animation:`. The dialogue window uses transitions only.
- **Registration & bundles**: Registered in `internal/desktop/types.go` (`games`, `open_maximized: "true"`, `logo_path: "/img/runenpfad/icon.svg"`), `desktop-foundation.js`, `module-loader.js`, `menus-and-routing.js`, and `window-shell-runtime.js`; bundled into `ui/js/desktop/bundles/main.bundle.js`.
