## R09 Brunos Arboretum: "Alma's Garden"

Scene: beautiful maintained disorder and specific traces of Alma: slanted handwriting on
seed jars, a wobbly chair, a leaning ladder. Bruno is allowed joy and sadness together;
the song cures nothing and says so. Keep it tender, never maudlin.
Current loop: `alma`, F Dorian, 72 BPM, forest day.

Style


Lyrics
```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Her handwriting is on the seed jars, slanted to the right
The ladder leans on the arbor in the evening light
A chair that wobbles gently, and nobody fixes it
She said a chair should have a mind, and a little bit of wit

[Chorus]
Both can grow here, both can grow
The grief and the garden, the gold and the slow
Sad and glad in the same warm light
Nothing's cured, and it's alright

[Verse 2]
I'll keep the wobbly chair, and I'll keep the crooked gate
I'll plant the seeds she labelled, and the others they can wait

I say, "Not today, thank you, I'm not finished yet"

[Chorus]

Nothing's cured, and it's alright

[Outro]
```
