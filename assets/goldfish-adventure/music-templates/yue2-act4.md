## R13 ALLGUT-Empfang: "Have a Pleasant Outcome"

Scene: the opening bureau's shapes perfected into sterile reassurance, apparent choices
that lead to the same result. ALLGUT is pleasant, never a villain with a speech. The song
is deliberately too perfect; the uncanny edge comes from the lyrics, not from menace in
the sound. The score header uses E major on purpose: the same tonic as the room's
E Phrygian loop, but cheerfully wrong.
Current loop: `a-useful-no`, E Phrygian, 80 BPM, space hum.

Title
```text
Have a Pleasant Outcome
```
Style
```text
English, immaculate cheerful corporate pop with an uncanny edge, glossy duet voices, bright celesta, clean plucked strings, soft kick drum, 80 BPM
```
Style (instrumental)
```text
immaculate cheerful corporate pop with an uncanny edge, bright celesta, clean plucked strings, soft kick drum, 80 BPM
```

```text
K:E
```
Lyrics
```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Welcome, welcome, welcome, we are so glad you are here
Every little worry has been cleared away, my dear
Please enjoy the lobby, please enjoy the light
Everything is satisfactory, and everything is right

[Chorus]
Have a pleasant outcome, have a pleasant day
Every road you take is the one we paved your way
Have a pleasant outcome, nothing to decide
All the doors are open and they all lead inside

[Verse 2]
Which door would you like? Door one, or door one?
Both are very lovely, and the choosing is done
Please do not remember what you came here to say

[Chorus]

[Outro]
```

## R14 Zustimmungstribunal: "One Approved Answer"

Scene: a huge chamber designed for one approved answer; the actual agreement and the
withdrawal clause are reconstructed here. Solemn and slow, with room to breathe. The
mood turns from echo to listening in the second verse.
Current loop: `a-useful-no`, E Phrygian, 80 BPM, space hum.

Title
```text
One Approved Answer
```
Style

Style (instrumental)

```text
Q:1/4=76
K:EPhr
```

Lyrics
```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
A hall built for one answer, and the echo knows the line
Every seat faces forward, every voice is asked to sign
The ceiling counts the yeses, and the floor repeats the vow
Nobody has a chance to ask what it means, and how

[Chorus]
Read it again, read it slow
Who said yes, and did they know?
Every word was written down
Every word still stands its ground

[Verse 2]
The record has a margin and the margin has a name
Someone wrote "withdraw at any time", and no one took the blame
The robot reads it out loud, and the chamber starts to listen
The margin note is shining and the long white pages glisten

[Chorus]
Read it again, read it slow
Who said yes, and did they know?
Every word was written down
Every word still stands its ground

[Outro]
Read it slow
```

```text
[Verse 1]
Wires in the open and a lever worn by dust
A manual switch that nobody was meant to trust
Three prepared protocols lie on a bench

[Chorus]
```
