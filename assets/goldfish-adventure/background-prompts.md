# Room background prompts

These final empty background plates were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The approved lounge concept is a style reference only. No provider/model ID is exposed. Actual dimensions and hashes are recorded in [background-manifest.json](background-manifest.json). Masters are preserved byte-for-byte in `masters/scenes/`; runtime copies are identical under `../../ui/img/goldfish-adventure/scenes/`. They are static plates, not evidence of layered scenery or authored animation. All 16 outputs are native 1672 × 941; the requested 1920 × 1080 was not returned, and no image was upscaled. Landmark rectangles are approximate normalized visual estimates for integration.
