## Cross-check
- TXS0108E: rates 110 Mbit/s push-pull and 1.2 Mbit/s open-drain from the own data sheet (Features and Table 5.9, VCCA 3.3 V / VCCB 5 V); TI SLYY165A also lists 110 Mbps. SCEA044 (2010) still says "in excess of 60 Mbps" and "down to 1.2 V"; the 2024 data sheet raises the rate and gives 1.4 V as VCCA minimum, so the data sheet wins. Pull-ups 4 k / 40 kΩ: data sheet = SCEA044.
- TXB0108: 100 Mbit/s data sheet = SLYY165A; push-pull only, > 50 kΩ, 70 pF: data sheet = SCEA043. Its `open_drain = no` is stated by TI itself.
- PCA9306: TI and NXP agree on auto-bidirectional, open-drain I/O, EN pin and the push-pull restriction, but give different supply ranges (TI 1.2 V min with VREF2 ≥ VREF1 + 0.6 V; NXP 1.0–3.6 V / 1.8–5.5 V) and different speed claims (TI "> 100 MHz", NXP "generally > 2 MHz", both RC/load dependent): both shown as text, numeric rate empty.
- LVC1T45: TI data sheet, Nexperia data sheet and TI SLYY165A agree on 420 Mbit/s (3.3 V → 5 V) and the lower rates. Supply minimum differs by maker (TI 1.65 V, Nexperia 1.2 V): both shown.
- LVC8T245: Nexperia data sheet 420 Mbit/s vs. TI SLYY165A 320 Mbit/s; the TI data sheet states none: empty.
- 74AHCT125: TI = Nexperia (VCC 4.5–5.5 V, VIH 2.0 V, VIL 0.8 V); 74HCT: TI SN74HCT125 (same input levels); method (3.3 V → 5 V via TTL inputs, extra ICC) TI SCEA035A = SLYY165A.
- 74LVC125A: TI = Nexperia (inputs to 5.5 V); VCC range differs (TI 1.65–3.6 V, Nexperia 1.2–3.6 V). The TI data sheet's section 7.3 says "Operates from 1.65V to 5.5V", its Recommended Operating Conditions say VCC 1.65–3.6 V; the table value is used.
- Discrete MOSFET: AN10441 is the method's own maker document (NXP 2007, re-issued unchanged by Nexperia 2020), so only one document; 400 kbit/s, the Hs-mode exclusion and VDD2 ≥ VDD1 come from it. TI SCEA035A section 4.2/7 describes the same principle for TVC FET arrays ("bidirectional … without direction control … requires external pullup resistors").
- Divider and series-resistor rows: Microchip DS41285A is the only maker document read that recommends them; no second maker document found.

## Deliberate deviations
- Rates are the best-case figures the makers print, with the supply pair in `rate_cond`; they are not guaranteed for every supply combination or load.
- `supply_a`/`supply_b` always mean the lower- and the higher-voltage side; for one-way single-supply methods they name the receiving or driving logic instead of A/B ports (74LVC125A: VCC is the lower side, the 5 V driver the higher side).
- LVC1T45 and LVC8T245 are split into two rows, because their rate evidence differs.
- 74AHCT125 and 74HCT share one row; the AHCT ΔICC figure in the TI data sheet (one input at 3.4 V) was not reliably readable from the text layout, so the remark quotes the SCEA035A plot value (HCT541) instead.
- `open_drain` is empty (not "no") for the one-way and direction-controlled rows (divider, series resistor, LVC1T45, LVC8T245, 74AHCT/HCT, 74LVC125A): no maker document states it. It is the researcher's own reasoning that a one-way or direction-controlled translator cannot by design pass a bidirectional open-drain bus such as I²C, and the shipped notes say so in those words. Only TXB0108 has a sourced "no".
- The research-only cross-check column is not shipped; its content is this section.

## UNVERIFIED (shipped empty)
- `open_drain` of divider, series_r, lvc1t45, lvc8t245, ttl_in and lvc_5vt: derived, no source.
- `mosfet.push_pull`: AN10441 covers open-drain I²C only.
- `pca9306.rate_od` / `rate_pp`: maker claims disagree (> 2 MHz vs. > 100 MHz).
- `lvc8t245.rate_pp`: 420 (Nexperia) vs. 320 Mbit/s (TI guide).
- Data rates for divider, series resistor, 74AHCT/HCT and 74LVC125A: no maker states one.
- Not covered: BSS138 and 2N7002 are not named in AN10441, which names only "a discrete N-channel enhancement MOSFET"; they appear in the table only as examples with their onsemi threshold voltages.

## Notes (en / de)
- en: Check both directions of every signal: an output's VOH/VOL must meet the receiver's VIH/VIL (see logic_levels), and no input may see more than its absolute maximum unless the data sheet calls it tolerant.
- de: Jedes Signal in beiden Richtungen prüfen: VOH/VOL des Ausgangs müssen VIH/VIL des Empfängers erfüllen (siehe logic_levels), und kein Eingang darf mehr als seinen Grenzwert sehen, außer das Datenblatt nennt ihn tolerant.
- en: Auto-direction translators (TXS, TXB) are sensitive to load, trace length and external resistors; TXB parts must not be used on I²C.
- de: Pegelwandler mit automatischer Richtungserkennung (TXS, TXB) reagieren empfindlich auf Last, Leitungslänge und externe Widerstände; TXB-Bausteine nicht an I²C verwenden.

## Suggested finder / lookups
- Filter by direction and bus type (open-drain / push-pull), then sort by `rate_pp`.
