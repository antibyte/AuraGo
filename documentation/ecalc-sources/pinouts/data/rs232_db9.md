# rs232_db9 — RS-232 (DE-9) / RS-232 (DE-9)

Kind: cross-checked reproductions
Pins: 9; types: signal 6, data 2, ground 1

## View
Written for the male connector, looking at its pins (`drawn: plug`): 1 2 3 4 5 on top, 6 7 8 9 below shifted half a pitch to the right, wide side of the D shell at the top.

Basis, quotes from the text of the sources:
- serial_pinout.txt: the drawing is labelled "Looking at pins on male connector" and shows `\1 2 3 4 5/` above `\6 7 8 9/`. It adds that on a female connector the numbers run right to left, starting with 1 in the upper right corner.
- CS Club Waterloo wiki: male "1 2 3 4 5" over "6 7 8 9", female "5 4 3 2 1" over "9 8 7 6".

The female order is exactly the mirror image the catalog schema's other view produces.

## Cross-check
TI, Moxa, serial_pinout.txt and CS Club agree on pins 1 to 8. TI, serial_pinout.txt and CS Club also agree on pin 9 (RI). Crypto Museum was used only for the male and female rule.

Directions: TI says for each signal who turns it on (DCE for DCD, DSR, CTS, RI; DTE for RTS, DTR; TD from DTE to DCE). serial_pinout.txt gives the same as arrows out of or into the PC. RD going into the DTE is only implicit in TI and explicit in serial_pinout.txt.

Disagreements:
- Moxa's table has pin 9 "Not assigned": that is that product, which has no RI. RI chosen.
- TI's figure 6 writes "RST" for pin 7, while its text says "Request to Send (RTS)". A typo in TI; RTS chosen.
- TI writes RD/TD, Moxa RxD/TxD, others RXD/TXD. The catalog schema's RXD/TXD is used.
- TI describes the genders by casing and contacts together: at the DTE a female casing with male contacts, at the DCE the reverse. Same meaning as "male pins at the DTE", but a reader looking at the casing could read it the other way round, so `doc` speaks of pins and sockets.

UNVERIFIED: TIA-232-F and TIA-574 themselves are paywalled and were not read.

## Choices
- `drawn: plug`, as in the catalog schema's example and because a PC's port is male.
- Direction is in `function`, for both ends.
- The note on crossing TXD and RXD between two DTEs is a consequence of the direction table. I name no specific null-modem wiring, because the handshake lines vary.
- The closing remark on levels stays general; TI's document covers the voltages, I give no numbers here.
- Connector name DE-9 in the title, DB9 in `doc` and in the tags, because most people search for that.
