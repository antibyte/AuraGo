## Choices
- Catalog item: `rows` 2x4 (`1 3 5 7` / `2 4 6 8`), `outline: rect`, `contact: blade`, `drawn: plug`, `key: left`, same views as part A. The item notes carry the one-sentence safety line, the footnote (never join the negative lines, never put them on ground), the explanation about bridged amplifiers (the record's own explanation, kept) and the pair layout. No `wire`.
- Names RR, FR, FL, RL with + and − (the standard says "rear right channel", "front right channel" etc.). `-` as a plain hyphen, as in the xlr item.
- `type: signal` (analogue audio), as for the audio items.
- `type: rows`, `outline: rect`, `contact: blade`, drawn view as above.
- No `wire`: the standard gives no colours.
- Recommended notes: "Each speaker has its own − line: never join the − lines or connect them to ground (ISO 10487-1, table 1 footnote 2: common negative wiring is not allowed). Bridged amplifier outputs can be destroyed by a − line on ground."  The second sentence is my explanation, not a source statement; keep or drop.

## Safety note
Speaker lines are low voltage, but a speaker − line shorted to the vehicle ground can destroy the radio's output stage. Disconnect the battery before working on the radio wiring.
