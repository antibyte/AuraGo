## Choices
- Catalog item: `circular`, `contact: round`, `drawn: socket`, two radii (0.35 and 0.75), the socket-face angles of the table above, listed in the table's order. No `key`: the rib and gap rest on a render only, so nothing is drawn (the gap of the outer ring at 6 o'clock shows anyway). The item notes say that the drawing follows Jaeger's figure, that the angles are approximate (about 7° from the maker's rendering, rounded to a 36° pattern) and that the contact numbers are exact; they also carry the one-sentence safety line, the separate returns of 9 and 10 and the 12 V scope. Contacts 11 and 13 have no `wire`: their core colours (white/black, white/red) have no id in `pinstyles.yaml`, and a pin note gives the colour in words. Contact 12 is `nc` without a wire.
- Names are short function names (no DIN designations: the Jaeger 13-pin table gives none).
- Lamp and supply contacts `power`, returns `ground`, 12 `nc` (Jaeger: "Reserved for future allocation", "No core").
- `wire` for all except 12 (Jaeger states "No core").
- `type: circular`, `contact: round`, `drawn: socket`, positions as in the table; two radii.
- `key: 180`: read from the plug render's rib and the gap of the outer ring, not from text. Keep only if the reviewer accepts a render as basis; otherwise omit.
- Recommended notes: "12 V system. Contacts 9 (permanent +) and 10 (ignition +) each have their own return (13 and 11); do not use contact 3 for them. Contact 9 is live with the ignition off." The last sentence follows from "steady" supply; the current rating of 9 and 10 is not stated by Jaeger.
- The 24 V variant of ISO 11446 is not covered.

## Safety note
Trailer lighting is road-safety equipment: a wrong contact can disable brake, indicator or tail lights. Contact 9 (permanent supply, 2.5 mm²) can feed caravan equipment and must be fused on the vehicle side; a short there can start a fire. Only the nominal 12 V and the cross-sections stated by Jaeger are given here; current ratings were not read. Wiring changes on the vehicle should be done by a competent person.
