## View
Written for the module seen from its component side (shield can or chip facing you), header at the left, antenna or SMA socket at the right: `drawn: socket` (equals the host's female header seen from above when the module plugs in face up).

Rows, top to bottom (left pin, right pin):
- [1 GND, 2 VCC]
- [3 CE, 4 CSN]
- [5 SCK, 6 MOSI]
- [7 MISO, 8 IRQ]

Odd pins in the column nearer the board edge, even pins nearer the chip. The other view (mirror) is the pin side seen from below.

Basis: Ebyte E01-ML01DP5 manual v4.5, page 2, "引脚定义" (pin definition) section: dimension drawing in plan view with the shield can drawn as a rectangle, the SMA socket to the right and the 2x4 header at the left marked ①② / ③④ / ⑤⑥ / ⑦⑧ from top to bottom; the side view under it shows the shield on top and the pins pointing down, so the plan view is the component side. Page 3 application schematic: module symbol "1 GND, 2 VCC, 3 CE, 4 CSN, 5 SCK, 6 MOSI, 7 MISO, 8 IRQ" with odd pins on the left and even pins on the right, consistent with the drawing.

## Cross-check
- Signal names: Ebyte's eight names are Nordic's chip pin names; Ebyte's directions (CE, CSN, SCK, MOSI in; MISO, IRQ out; IRQ active low) agree with Nordic's table 1.
- Supply: Ebyte 2.0 to 3.6 V for the PA/LNA module, Nordic 1.9 to 3.6 V for the chip. The pin text uses Ebyte's module range and the item note names both.
- 5 V logic: Nordic says the inputs are 5 V tolerant only with VDD between 2.7 and 3.3 V; Ebyte allows "0.7VCC ~ 5V" and still advises series resistors for 5 V lines. The note therefore says: VCC never above 3.6 V, 5 V signal lines only with care.
- Only one module maker's document was read. The header order there is maker data for Ebyte's E01 family only.

## Choices
- Catalog item: named "nRF24L01 module (2×4)" and described as the common header as documented for the Ebyte E01 modules; the signal names are Nordic's. The note says that unbranded modules have no maker document and that the supply is 1.9 to 3.6 V per Nordic against 2.0 to 3.6 V stated by the module maker. `rows` with four rows of two, `outline: rect`, `contact: square`, `drawn: socket`.
- CSN is written "SPI chip select, input" without "active low": Nordic's table 1 does not contain the word; active low is the SPI convention, but it is not a statement of either source. IRQ "active low" is stated by both.
- CE and IRQ `signal`, SPI lines `data`.
- The "more than 100 mA when sending" of the note is Ebyte's 130 mA at 20 dBm for a PA/LNA module.
