# tip120 — TIP120 NPN Darlington (TO-220) / TIP120 NPN-Darlington (TO-220)

Kind: authoritative maker document (onsemi); no second maker readable
Pins: 3; types: signal 3

## Part
TIP120, 60 V / 5 A NPN Darlington power transistor in TO-220AB (onsemi CASE 221A). Suggested display name
`TIP120 (TO-220)`. onsemi's sheet covers TIP120, TIP121, TIP122 (NPN) and TIP125, TIP126, TIP127 (PNP) with the same
case and style; the PNP types are not part of this item.

## Pin table
| No. | Name | Function (en) | Function (de) | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | B | Base | Basis | signal |
| 2 | C | Collector, also on the tab | Kollektor, auch an der Metallfahne | signal |
| 3 | E | Emitter | Emitter | signal |

Tab ("4" in onsemi's figure): collector.

Suggested item note (en/de): "The metal tab is connected to the collector (pin 2): a heat sink on the tab carries the
collector voltage, so insulate it from metal at any other potential." / "Die Metallfahne ist mit dem Kollektor (Pin 2)
verbunden: Ein Kühlkörper an der Fahne führt die Kollektorspannung, also gegen Metall mit anderem Potenzial isolieren."
(Insulation advice is my conclusion from "4. Collector".)

## View
Shape `to`, package `to220`: label side towards the viewer, leads down, pins 1, 2, 3 left to right. Basis:
- onsemi page 1: picture of the part with the black plastic body towards the viewer, tab (labelled "4") behind and above
  it, leads "1 2 3" from left to right; legend "1. Base, 2. Collector, 3. Emitter, 4. Collector"; "TO−220AB CASE 221A
  STYLE 1".
- onsemi case 221A outline (ISSUE AL, DATE 05 FEB 2025, PDF page 8): the front drawing (plastic body below the tab with
  its hole) numbers the leads "1 2 3" from left to right; the rear drawing (metal side) numbers them "3 2 1". So pin 1
  is on the left when the plastic/label side faces the viewer — the catalog view. Style list: STYLE 1 = pin 1 base,
  2 collector, 3 emitter, 4 collector.
I rendered pages 1 and 8 to images and looked at them.
