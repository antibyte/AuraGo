# sn74hc08 — SN74HC08 quad 2-input AND gate (DIP-14) / SN74HC08 vierfach AND-Gatter mit 2 Eingängen (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
SN74HC08N: four independent 2-input AND gates in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP, 14" in the Device Information table). Suggested display name `SN74HC08 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC08 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC08 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's pin figure and the D/DB/N/PW/J/W column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC08A and MC74HCT08A: one document and one pinout figure for both, and its page 1 says "The MC74HC08A/MC74HCT08A is identical in pinout to the LS08".
- Not claimed: the SN74HC08 in SO (NS). TI's Device Information table lists "NS (SOP, 14)", but the pin figure caption and the table column leave NS out.
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC08.

Suggested item note (read from the table above; the same order as the shipped sn74hc00 item): gates 1 and 2 run A, B, Y by rising pin number (pins 1 to 6). Gates 3 and 4 are the other way round: the output has the lowest number, 3Y is pin 8 (3A 9, 3B 10) and 4Y is pin 11 (4A 12, 4B 13). The pins sit where the 74HC00 has them. / Bei Gatter 1 und 2 folgen A, B, Y mit steigender Pin-Nummer (Pins 1 bis 6). Bei Gatter 3 und 4 ist es umgekehrt: Der Ausgang hat die kleinste Nummer, 3Y ist Pin 8 (3A 9, 3B 10) und 4Y ist Pin 11 (4A 12, 4B 13). Die Pins liegen dort, wo sie auch beim 74HC00 liegen.

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: the pin figure in TI section 4 (no figure number in this revision) is captioned "D, DB, N, PW, J, or W Package", "14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, TSSOP, CDIP, or CFP" and "Top View". It shows 1–7 down the left (1A, 1B, 1Y, 2A, 2B, 2Y, GND) and 14–8 down the right (VCC, 4B, 4A, 4Y, 3B, 3A, 3Y), with a small circle inside the body next to pin 1 as the pin 1 mark; no notch. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at the figure. onsemi's "Pinout: 14-Lead Packages (Top View)" draws the package lying on its side with the notch at the pin 1 / pin 14 end and the same labels.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins. Inside TI's document the pin figure, the Pin Functions table and the "Functional Pinout" drawing on page 1 agree. Inside onsemi's document the logic diagram (A1 1, B1 2, Y1 3 … A3 9, B3 10, Y3 8, A4 12, B4 13, Y4 11, "PIN 14 = VCC", "PIN 7 = GND") and the pinout drawing agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Naming order: TI `1A`, `1Y`; onsemi `A1`, `Y1`.
- Packages: onsemi lists SOIC-14 NB and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both (onsemi gives 4.5 V to 5.5 V for the HCT version).

## Choices
- Names follow TI, the primary source. No pin carries an overbar.
- All gate inputs and outputs are `signal`.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V. The supply table of this revision prints the 6 V maximum in its own column; I read it in the text layer as "Supply voltage 2 5 6 V".
- The 20-pin FK package is left out.
- Section numbers in this TI revision are one lower than in SCLS076G (pins are section 4, ratings section 5).
