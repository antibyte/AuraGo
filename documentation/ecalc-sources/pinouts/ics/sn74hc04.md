# sn74hc04 — SN74HC04 hex inverter (DIP-14) / SN74HC04 sechsfach Inverter (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
SN74HC04N: six independent inverters in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (14)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC04 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC04 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC04 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's Figure 5-1 and the 14-pin column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC04A and MC74HCT04A: one pinout figure for both. The same page says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS04 and the MC14069".
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC04.

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's "Figure 5-1. D, DB, N, NS, PW, J, or W Package, 14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP, CDIP, or CFP, Top View" shows 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right. This figure draws neither a notch nor a pin 1 dot; the orientation follows from the numbers and the caption. onsemi's "Figure 2. Pinout (Top View)" shows the notch at the pin 1 / pin 14 end, which confirms it. I rendered TI PDF page 3 and onsemi page 1 to images and looked at both figures.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins. Inside TI's document Figure 5-1 and the Pin Functions table agree on all 14 pins; inside onsemi's document the logic diagram (with pin numbers) and the pinout figure agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Naming order: TI `1A`, `1Y`; onsemi `A1`, `Y1`.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP. It confirms the 14-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both (onsemi: 4.5 V to 5.5 V for the HCT version, which is not this item).

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names are TI's, as in the table above (`1A`, `1Y`).
- Names follow TI (`1A`, `1Y`), the primary source.
- No pin carries an overbar. For the record: the existing `sn74hc595` item drops overbars and writes bare names (`OE`, `SRCLR`) with "active low" in the function text; the other records of this batch mark an overbar with a leading `~`.
- All inverter pins are `signal`.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3).
- TI's table says "Channel 1, Input A"; the function text says "Inverter 1, input".
- TI's caption of Figure 5-1 reads "ph Top View": the "ph" is a leftover in TI's document and means nothing.
- The 20-pin FK package is left out.
