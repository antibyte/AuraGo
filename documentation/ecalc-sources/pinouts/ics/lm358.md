# lm358 — LM358 dual op-amp (DIP-8) / LM358 Doppel-OPV (DIP-8)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 8; types: power 2, signal 6

## Members that share this pinout
- LM158, LM258, LM2904 and LM358 in the other 8-pin packages of the same sheet.
- TL072, TL072A, TL072B and TL072H (JFET input): TI SLOS080W, Figure 4-5 "TL072x D, DDF, JG, P, PS, and PW Packages" and Table 4-3 give 1OUT, 1IN-, 1IN+, VCC-, 2IN+, 2IN-, 2OUT, VCC+ on pins 1 to 8, the same functions as this item. Not covered: the TL072M in the 10-pin CFP (U) and 20-pin LCCC (FK), which TI draws separately with other numbers.
- NE5532 and NE5532A (low noise; SA5532 and SA5532A too): TI SLOS075K, Figure 4-1 (D, P and PS packages) and Pin Functions give the same eight functions (the figure labels pin 1 `1OUT`, the table `OUT1`).
- Not claimed: the TL082 and other look-alikes; no document read for them.

## View
Shape `dip`, 8 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 4-1 is captioned for the 8-pin packages including PDIP (package P) and ends in "Top View". The figure marks pin 1 with a dot at the top left and runs 1–4 down the left and 8–5 down the right. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at it. The figure has a dot, not a notch; the drawing rule of the `dip` shape is the same.

## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure and Table 4-1 agree on all 8 pins. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED. In each of the TL072 and NE5532 sheets the figure and the table agree on every pin. The note says nothing about the input and output ranges of the two parts (not read for the NE5532).

## Choices
- `V-` is typed `power`, not `ground`: TI calls it the negative (lowest) supply, or ground in single-supply use, and a split-supply circuit has a real negative rail there. The function text states both uses.
- Pin names use ASCII minus (`IN1-`, `V-`) where TI prints an en dash, so that a search with a keyboard minus finds them.
- The 20-pin FK package is left out.
