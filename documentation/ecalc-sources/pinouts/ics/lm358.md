# lm358 — LM358 dual op-amp (DIP-8) / LM358 Doppel-OPV (DIP-8)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 8; types: power 2, signal 6

## View
Shape `dip`, 8 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 4-1 is captioned for the 8-pin packages including PDIP (package P) and ends in "Top View". The figure marks pin 1 with a dot at the top left and runs 1–4 down the left and 8–5 down the right. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at it. The figure has a dot, not a notch; the drawing rule of the `dip` shape is the same.

## Sources
1. Texas Instruments, LM158, LM258, LM2904, LM358 Industry-Standard Dual Operational Amplifiers, SLOS068AB (June 1976, revised October 2024), section 4, Figure 4-1 and Table 4-1. https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm358.pdf — covers LM358 in P (PDIP, 8), read PDF page 3, accessed 2026-11-04.

## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure and Table 4-1 agree on all 8 pins. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED.

## Choices
- `V-` is typed `power`, not `ground`: TI calls it the negative (lowest) supply, or ground in single-supply use, and a split-supply circuit has a real negative rail there. The function text states both uses.
- Pin names use ASCII minus (`IN1-`, `V-`) where TI prints an en dash, so that a search with a keyboard minus finds them.
- The 20-pin FK package is left out.
