# lm317 — LM317 adjustable regulator (TO-220) / LM317 einstellbarer Spannungsregler (TO-220)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 3; types: power 2, signal 1

## View
Shape `to`, package `to220`: label side towards the viewer, leads pointing down, pins left to right `1`, `2`, `3`. Basis: TI's Figure 5-2 is captioned "KCS or KCT Package, 3-Pin TO-220 (Top View), Legacy Chip". It draws the package on its side, tab with hole on the left and leads to the right, with 3 INPUT at the top, 2 OUTPUT in the middle, 1 ADJUST at the bottom. Turning that drawing 90° clockwise, so that the leads point down, moves the top lead to the right: left to right 1 ADJUST, 2 OUTPUT, 3 INPUT. A top view of a TO-220 looks at the plastic side, not the metal back, which is the catalog schema's label side. I rendered PDF page 5 to an image and looked at it.

## Sources
1. Texas Instruments, LM317 3-Pin Adjustable Regulator, SLVS044Z (September 1997, revised April 2025), section 5, Figure 5-2 and table Pin Functions, Metal Can Packages. https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm317.pdf — covers TO-220 (KCS, KCT), read PDF page 5, accessed 2026-11-04.

## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure 5-2 and the table agree: ADJUST 1, INPUT 3, OUTPUT 2 and TAB. This confirms the order given in the task. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED.

## Choices
- Names follow TI's table (`ADJUST`, `OUTPUT`, `INPUT`); `adj` is a tag.
- `OUTPUT` and `INPUT` are `power` by the catalog schema ("any supply rail, input or output"); `ADJUST` is `signal`.
- The tab: TI's table lists OUTPUT as "2, TAB" and the figure labels the tab OUTPUT. It goes in the item's notes and the function of pin 2. The advice to insulate the heat sink is my own conclusion from that connection, not a sentence of the datasheet.
- TI's TO-220 column is headed "Legacy Chip"; the datasheet lists no other TO-220 pinout, and the KCS and KCT packages are the ones it covers. Makers other than TI were not checked.
