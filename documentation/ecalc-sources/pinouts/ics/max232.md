# max232 — MAX232 dual RS-232 driver/receiver (DIP-16) / MAX232 doppelter RS-232-Treiber/Empfänger (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker document (Texas Instruments). Part: MAX232 dual EIA-232 driver and receiver with a charge pump that makes the ±RS-232 levels from one 5 V supply, in the 16-pin PDIP (TI package N), 16 pins. TI's figure is captioned for MAX232 in D, DW, N and NS and for MAX232I in D, DW and N, so the MAX232I shares the pinout. Second maker NOT read (see UNVERIFIED).
Pins: 16; types: power 3, ground 1, signal 4, data 8

Suggested item note (read, TI sections 8.1, 8.2 and Figure 8-1): the chip needs four 1 µF capacitors: C1 between pins 1 and 3, C2 between pins 4 and 5, C3 from pin 2 to GND or VCC, C4 from pin 6 to GND, plus a 1 µF bypass on VCC. With polarized capacitors, mind the polarity drawn in Figure 8-1 (C4's plus side goes to GND). Pins 9 to 12 go to the UART or logic, pins 7, 8, 13 and 14 to the RS-232 connector. The logic signals are inverted: a high at TIN gives a low (negative) RS-232 level. An open receiver input reads as a high at ROUT. / Der Baustein braucht vier 1-µF-Kondensatoren: C1 zwischen Pin 1 und 3, C2 zwischen Pin 4 und 5, C3 von Pin 2 nach GND oder VCC, C4 von Pin 6 nach GND, dazu 1 µF zum Abblocken an VCC. Bei gepolten Kondensatoren auf die Polung in Figure 8-1 achten (bei C4 liegt der Pluspol an GND). Pin 9 bis 12 gehen zum UART oder zur Logik, Pin 7, 8, 13 und 14 zum RS-232-Stecker. Die Logiksignale werden invertiert: high an TIN ergibt einen low-Pegel (negativ) auf RS-232. Ein offener Empfängereingang wird an ROUT als high gelesen.

## Cross-check
One maker document only (see UNVERIFIED). Inside TI's document:
- Figure 4-1 and Table 4-1 agree on the 15 pins the table lists. Table 4-1 has no row for pin 12: it goes from T1IN (11) to R1IN (13). Pin 12 `R1OUT` is read from Figure 4-1, and confirmed by Figure 8-1 (pin 12 is the "To CMOS or TTL" output of the receiver whose input is pin 13) and by section 8.1 ("Pins 9 through 12 connect to UART or general purpose logic lines").
- Figure 8-1 pairs driver input 11 with output 14, input 10 with output 7, receiver input 13 with output 12, input 8 with output 9, and shows C1 on pins 1/3, C2 on 4/5, C3 on pin 2, C4 on pin 6, VCC on 16 and GND on 15. All agree with Figure 4-1.

## Choices
- Catalog item: VS+ and VS- are typed `power` (generated rails, storage capacitor only), the capacitor pins C1+, C1-, C2+, C2- `signal`; pin 12 `R1OUT` rests on TI's Figure 4-1 and Figure 8-1 because TI's Table 4-1 omits it, and the item notes say so; the notes also carry the capacitor list, the inversion and the +-30 V receiver inputs.
- Types: the four TTL/CMOS pins and the four RS-232 line pins are `data` (serial RS-232 data, as planned). VS+ and VS− are `power` because they are the generated ±8.5 V rails (TI type O, "for storage capacitor only"); the function text says that only the storage capacitor belongs there. The flying-capacitor pins C1± and C2± are typed `signal`, not `power`, so that the page does not suggest wiring a supply to them; `power` would also be defensible.
- `VS-`, `C1-`, `C2-` use the ASCII hyphen as TI's table does.
- Supply: TI section 5.3, VCC 4.5 V to 5.5 V (nominal 5 V); section 8.2.1 repeats "VCC minimum is 4.5V and maximum is 5.5V". Absolute maximum VCC 6 V (section 5.1).
- Receiver inputs accept ±30 V (section 5.1 and 5.3, and page 1). Not put in the pin text; could go in the item notes.
- "About ±8.5 V" on pins 2 and 6 is read from Figure 8-1 (arrows labelled 8.5 V and −8.5 V). TI section 5.1 limits VS+ to 15 V and VS− to −15 V absolute.
- The inversion statement is read in section 7.4.3: Table 7-1 "Each Driver" reads TIN L → TOUT H, H → L (page 9); Table 7-2 "Each Receiver" reads RIN L → ROUT H, H → L, Open → H (page 10).
- C3 "to GND or VCC": Figure 8-1 footnote "† C3 can be connected to VCC or GND". Polarity of C4 (plus to GND) is read from the "+" mark in Figure 8-1; note B says nonpolarized ceramic capacitors are acceptable.
