# cd4051 — CD4051B 8-channel analog multiplexer (DIP-16) / CD4051B 8-Kanal-Analogmultiplexer (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 16; types: power 2, ground 1, signal 4, io 9

## Part
CD4051BE: CMOS single 8-channel analog multiplexer/demultiplexer with three binary select inputs, an inhibit input and a separate negative supply for the analog path, in the 16-pin plastic DIP (TI: "N (PDIP, 16)" in the package table, "E (PDIP)" in the figure caption and the thermal table). Suggested display name `CD4051B (DIP-16)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- CD4051B in CDIP, SOIC, SOP and TSSOP: TI's Figure 4-1 is captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC14051B: its page 1 says "Pin-for-Pin Replacement for CD4051, CD4052, and CD4053", and its pin assignment has the same numbers.
- Not this pinout: the CD4052B (dual 4-channel) and CD4053B (triple 2-channel) of the same TI document. Their switch pins are arranged differently, and the CD4052B has its select inputs on other pins (TI Figures 4-2 and 4-3).
- 74HC4051: onsemi's page 1 points to the "HC4051" for lower on-resistance but makes no pinout statement, so not claimed.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 16 pins: channels 4, 6 on pins 1 and 2, common on 3, channels 7, 5 on pins 4 and 5, inhibit on 6, VEE on 7, VSS on 8, selects C, B, A on 9, 10, 11, channels 3, 0, 1, 2 on pins 12 to 15, VDD on 16. The two truth tables agree: inhibit low and C B A = 000 to 111 switch on channel 0 to 7, inhibit high switches on none. Inside TI's document Figure 4-1, Table 4-1 and Figure 7-1 agree on all 16 pins; inside onsemi's document the function drawing, the pin assignment and Figure 2 agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Channel names: TI `CH 0 IN/OUT` to `CH 7 IN/OUT` and `COM OUT/IN`; onsemi `X0` to `X7` and `X`.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-16 and TSSOP-16, no DIP. It confirms the 16-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Supply: TI states 3 V to 20 V single and ±3 V to ±10 V dual (features, page 1) and an absolute maximum of −0.5 V to 20 V (section 5.1). onsemi states a supply range of 3.0 V to 18 V and an absolute maximum of −0.5 V to +18.0 V for the MC14051B. So TI's upper working figure equals its own absolute maximum, and lies above onsemi's limit.
- VEE rule: onsemi says "VEE must be ≤ VSS" (pages 1 and 2). TI does not state it as a rule in the pages read; its four bias examples in Figure 6-1 all have VEE at or below VSS.

Oddity inside TI's document, not touching the pins: the package letters do not match between places. The package table on page 1 lists the plastic DIP as "N (PDIP, 16)" and the SOIC as "D"; the caption of Figure 4-1 and the thermal table call them "E" and "M". The part is the same, and the figure names the PDIP either way.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names are TI's, as in the table above. The supply ranges, which differ between TI and onsemi, are in the item note, not in a pin function.
- Names follow TI's Table 4-1 exactly, with the spaces (`CH 4 IN/OUT`, `COM OUT/IN`). They are long for a pin label; onsemi's `X4` and `X` would be shorter, but TI is the primary source and its names say what the pin does.
- No overbars on this part: `INH` is an active-high inhibit. For the record: the existing `sn74hc595` item drops overbars and writes bare names (`OE`, `SRCLR`) with "active low" in the function text; the other records of this batch mark an overbar with a leading `~`.
- Types: the eight channels and the common pin are `io`, because the switches pass the analog signal in both directions. INH, A, B and C are `signal`. `VDD` and `VEE` are both `power` (TI: "Positive power input", "Negative power input"); `VSS` is `ground` (TI: "Ground"). This makes two power pins.
- Pin 16 text gives no voltage range, unlike the logic ICs of this batch. The two makers differ (20 V and 18 V), TI's 20 V is at the same time its absolute maximum, and the meaning depends on where VEE sits. The ranges are in the suggested item note with their source instead.
- "the low level of the control inputs" for VSS is read from the note under TI's Figure 6-1 ("logic levels are: 0 = VSS and 1 = VDD") and from onsemi page 2 ("Control Inputs referenced to VSS").
- The CD4052B and CD4053B are left out.
