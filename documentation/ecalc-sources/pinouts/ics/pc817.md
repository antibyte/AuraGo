# pc817 — Sharp PC817 optocoupler (DIP-4) / Sharp PC817 Optokoppler (DIP-4)

Kind: authoritative maker document (Sharp)
Pins: 4; types: signal 4 (see Choices)

## Part
Sharp PC817XxNSZ1B series (the current PC817 type: IRED optically coupled to a phototransistor, 4-pin DIP, Viso(rms)
5 kV, VCEO 80 V, CTR 50 % to 400 % at IF = 5 mA, VCE = 5 V). UL/CSA files list it "as model No. PC817". Suggested display
name `PC817 (DIP-4)`. Sharp's product lineup page "DIP 4pin" links this datasheet.

Members that share this pinout, as far as the datasheet says so: the through-hole version (ex. PC817XNNSZ1B) and the
SMT gull-wing lead form (ex. PC817XNNIP1B) — the Outline section draws both with the same pin numbers.

sides are isolated from each other. Pin 1 is marked by a dot on the top. The LED needs a series resistor." /
"Eingangsseite (LED) an Pin 1 und 2, Ausgangsseite (Fototransistor) an Pin 3 und 4; beide Seiten sind voneinander
isoliert. Pin 1 ist oben mit einem Punkt markiert. Die LED braucht einen Vorwiderstand."
(The series-resistor sentence is general practice, not a datasheet sentence; "Anode mark" is Sharp's label for the dot.)

## View
Shape `dip`, 4 pins: package from above, notch/mark at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise (1 top left,
2 bottom left, 3 bottom right, 4 top right). Basis: Sharp page 2, "Outline 1. Through-Hole [ex. PC817XNNSZ1B]": the top of
the package with the readable marking "PC817 S", the "Anode mark" dot next to pin ①, leads ① (upper) and ② (lower) on
the left side, ④ (upper) and ③ (lower) on the right side. The marking is readable, so it is drawn from above; pin 1 at
the upper left and counter-clockwise numbering match the catalog's DIP view (the dot takes the place of a notch).
The "Internal Connection Diagram" on the same page puts ① ② on the left (LED) and ④ ③ on the right (transistor) in
the same positions. I rendered page 2 to an image and looked at it.

## Cross-check
- Inside Sharp's document: the connection diagram's legend, its drawing and both outline drawings agree on all four
  pins.
- One maker only: PC817 is Sharp's part number. Compatible parts from other makers carry other names (e.g. Lite-On
  LTV-817, Everlight EL817) and were not read.

## Choices
- Catalog item: name `PC817 (DIP-4)` in all locales; names are Sharp's legend; all four pins `signal`; the note says input side 1 and 2, output side 3 and 4, dot at pin 1, series resistor, and that the SMT gull-wing version has the same numbers; the dot takes the place of the DIP notch in the drawing.
- Names as in Sharp's legend (`Anode`, `Cathode`, `Emitter`, `Collector`); all within 24 characters.
- All four typed `signal`: none is a supply rail. (Typing the LED pins `power`/`ground` would wrongly suggest supply
  pins.)
- The SMT gull-wing version is mentioned only in the notes; the item draws the DIP.
