# cd4013 — CD4013B dual D flip-flop with set and reset (DIP-14) / CD4013B zweifaches D-Flipflop mit Setz- und Rücksetzeingang (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi and Renesas)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 4, data 8

## Part
CD4013BE: CMOS dual positive-edge-triggered D flip-flop with asynchronous, active-high set and reset, in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI Device Information: CD4013BE = PDIP (14)). Suggested display name `CD4013B (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- CD4013B in SOIC (D), CDIP (J), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW): TI's pin figure is captioned "D, J, N, NS, PW Package", "14-Pin SOIC, CDIP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP". TI's Device Information lists CD4013BE, CD4013BF, CD4013BM, CD4013BNS and CD4013BPW for PDIP, CDIP, SOIC, SO and TSSOP.
- onsemi MC14013B: its page 1 says "Pin-for-Pin Replacement for CD4013B", and its pin assignment has the same numbers.
- Renesas (formerly Intersil/Harris) CD4013BMS: same numbers and names in its "Pinout" and "Functional Diagram".
- Not this pinout: the 74HC74 (see note).

Suggested item note (TI section 7.1 and Table 1; onsemi page 1 for the last sentence): unlike the 74HC74, SET and RESET are active high: hold both low for clocked operation. If both are high, Q and ~Q are both high. Flip-flop 2 repeats the order of flip-flop 1 from pin 13 down to pin 8 (Q, ~Q, CLOCK, RESET, D, SET), so it sits one row lower: Q2 on pin 13 faces ~Q1 on pin 2. The pinout is not that of the 74HC74. Tie unused inputs to VSS or VDD. / Anders als beim 74HC74 sind SET und RESET aktiv high: Für den getakteten Betrieb beide auf low halten. Sind beide high, sind Q und ~Q beide high. Flipflop 2 wiederholt die Reihenfolge von Flipflop 1 von Pin 13 abwärts bis Pin 8 (Q, ~Q, CLOCK, RESET, D, SET) und liegt damit eine Reihe tiefer: Q2 an Pin 13 liegt gegenüber von ~Q1 an Pin 2. ## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's figure in section 5 is captioned "D, J, N, NS, PW Package", "14-Pin SOIC, CDIP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP" and "Top View". It shows 1–7 down the left (Q1, Q1-bar, CLOCK1, RESET1, D1, SET1, VSS) and 14–8 down the right (VDD, Q2, Q2-bar, CLOCK2, RESET2, D2, SET2), with a small circle inside the body at the pin 1 corner as the pin 1 mark; no notch ("Not to scale"). I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at the figure. onsemi's "PIN ASSIGNMENT" draws the package upright with a dot at pin 1; Renesas' "Pinout" draws it with a notch at the top. Both show the same labels at the same pins.

## Cross-check
All three sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins. Inside TI's document the pin figure, the Pin Functions table and the functional block diagram (SET1 6, D1 5, CLOCK1 3, RESET1 4, Q1-bar 2, Q1 1; SET2 8, D2 9, CLOCK2 11, RESET2 10, Q2-bar 12, Q2 13) agree; inside onsemi's document the pin assignment and the block diagram ("VDD = PIN 14", "VSS = PIN 7") agree; Renesas' pinout and functional diagram agree.

Function: TI Table 1 and onsemi's truth table agree: a rising clock edge copies D to Q while set and reset are low; reset high gives Q = 0, set high gives Q = 1, both high give Q = 1 and Q-bar = 1. TI: "Setting or resetting is independent of the clock and is accomplished by a high level on the set or reset line".

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Names: TI `Q1`, `CLOCK1`, `RESET1`, `D1`, `SET1`; onsemi `QA`, `CA`, `RA`, `DA`, `SA` (subscript letters); Renesas `CLOCK 1`, `RESET 1`, `SET 1` with a space.
- Packages: onsemi lists SOIC-14, SOEIAJ-14 and TSSOP-14, no DIP; Renesas' page 1 shows no plastic DIP. TI's figure is the only one of the three that names the PDIP.
- Supply: recommended 3 V to 18 V in TI (section 6.3) and onsemi (page 1). Absolute maximum: TI −0.5 V to 20 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +18.0 V.

## Choices
- Names follow TI, the primary source.
- Overbars: TI prints an overbar over `Q1` and `Q2` at pins 2 and 12; this record writes `~Q1` and `~Q2`. The `~` must stay in the catalog: without it pins 1 and 2 would both be named `Q1`, and pins 12 and 13 both `Q2` (same reason as `~QH` in the shipped `sn74hc165` item). SET and RESET carry no overbar: they are active high.
- Types: D, CLOCK, Q and Q-bar are `data`, SET and RESET `signal`, the same split as the `sn74hc74` record of this batch (register precedent of the shipped `sn74hc595` and `sn74hc165` items). All twelve logic pins `signal` would also be defensible.
- Pin 14 text uses the recommended range 3 V to 18 V of TI and onsemi, not TI's 20 V absolute maximum.
- TI calls the flip-flops "Channel 1" and "Channel 2"; the function text says "Flip-flop".
- "Tie unused inputs to VSS or VDD" rests on onsemi page 1 ("Unused inputs must be tied to an appropriate logic voltage level (e.g., either VSS or VDD)").
- The 74HC74 comparison in the note rests on the `sn74hc74` record of this batch: there preset and clear are active low and pin 1 is ~1CLR, not an output.
- Catalog names: `~Q1` and `~Q2` keep their `~`; no other pin has an overbar.
