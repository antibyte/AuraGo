# sn74hc139 — SN74HC139 dual 2-to-4 line decoder/demultiplexer (DIP-16) / SN74HC139 zweifacher 2-zu-4-Decoder/Demultiplexer (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 14

## Part
SN74HC139N: two independent 2-line to 4-line decoders/demultiplexers, each with one active-low enable (strobe) input and four active-low outputs, in the 16-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (16)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC139 (DIP-16)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC139 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC139 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's pin figure is captioned "J , W, D, DB, N, NS, OR PW Package", "16-Pin CDIP, CFP, SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP".
- onsemi MC74HC139A: same numbering; page 1 says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS139".
- 74HCT139: not stated in either document, so not claimed.
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC139.

Suggested item note (TI section 7.3 and page 1): with ~G low, B and A pick one output as a binary number, A is the lowest bit, and that output goes low while the other three stay high; with ~G high all four are high. The second decoder mirrors the first from pin 15 down to pin 9 (~2G, 2A, 2B, 2Y0 to 2Y3), one row lower: ~2G on pin 15 faces 1A on pin 2. As a demultiplexer, ~G is the data input. / Bei ~G low wählen B und A als Binärzahl einen Ausgang, A ist das niedrigste Bit; dieser Ausgang geht auf low, die anderen drei bleiben high. Bei ~G high sind alle vier high. Der zweite Decoder wiederholt die Belegung des ersten von Pin 15 abwärts bis Pin 9 (~2G, 2A, 2B, 2Y0 bis 2Y3) und liegt eine Reihe tiefer: ~2G an Pin 15 liegt gegenüber von 1A an Pin 2. Als Demultiplexer ist ~G der Dateneingang.

## View

Basis: TI's pin figure in section 4 (no figure number) is captioned "J , W, D, DB, N, NS, OR PW Package", "16-Pin CDIP, CFP, SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP" and "Top View". It draws the notch at the top, 1–8 down the left (1G, 1A, 1B, 1Y0, 1Y1, 1Y2, 1Y3, GND) and 16–9 down the right (VCC, 2G, 2A, 2B, 2Y0, 2Y1, 2Y2, 2Y3), with an overbar on the G of 1G and 2G. ## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 16 pins: enable 1 and 15, select inputs 2 and 3 (decoder 1) and 14 and 13 (decoder 2) with the lowest bit on 2 and 14, outputs 0 to 3 on 4 to 7 and on 12 down to 9, GND 8, VCC 16. Inside onsemi's document the pin assignment and Figure 1 (with pin numbers, "PIN 16 = VCC", "PIN 8 = GND") agree. The function tables agree: TI (section 7.3) G high gives all outputs high; with G low, B A = L L, L H, H L, H H gives Y0, Y1, Y2, Y3 low. onsemi's table gives the same with Select, A1, A0. Bit order: TI's table puts B as the high bit and A as the low bit; onsemi names them A1 and A0.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Names: TI `1G` (overbar), `1A`, `1B`, `1Y0` to `1Y3`; onsemi `SELECTa`, `A0a`, `A1a`, `Y0a` to `Y3a` (b for the second decoder). onsemi's pin assignment prints no overbar; its logic diagram draws inversion circles on SELECT and on all outputs and calls the outputs "ACTIVE-LOW OUTPUTS".
- TI's own Functional Block Diagram (page 1, section 7.2) labels the select inputs `xA0` and `xA1` instead of the `xA` and `xB` of its pin figure, and shows no pin numbers despite its note "Pin numbers shown are for the D, DB, J, N, NS, PW, W". The pin figure is taken as the naming source.
- Packages: onsemi lists SOIC-16, TSSOP-16 and QFN16, no DIP.
- Supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V absolute, 2 V to 6 V recommended; onsemi's page 1 gives 2.0 V to 6.0 V as operating range.

## Choices
- Names follow TI's pin figure, the primary source.
- Overbars: TI prints an overbar on G at pins 1 and 15; this record writes `~1G` and `~2G`. If the catalog strips overbars as in the shipped `sn74hc138` item, write `1G` and `2G`; the function text already says "active low". The outputs are active low but TI gives `1Y0` to `2Y3` no overbar, so they get no `~` (as in sn74hc138).
- Function text calls the units "Decoder 1" and "Decoder 2"; TI says "channel".
- All logic pins are `signal`, select inputs included, as in the shipped `sn74hc138` item.
- Pin 16 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 5.2).
- "As a demultiplexer, ~G is the data input" rests on TI section 7.1 ("The active-low enable (G) input can be used as a data line in demultiplexing applications") and onsemi page 1 ("utilizing the Select as a data input").
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
- Catalog names: the overbars are dropped (`1G`, `2G`) with "active low" in the function text, as in `sn74hc138`.
