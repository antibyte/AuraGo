# sn74hc161 — SN74HC161 and SN74HC163 4-bit synchronous binary counters (DIP-16) / SN74HC161 und SN74HC163 synchrone 4-Bit-Binärzähler (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 6, data 8

## Part
SN74HC161N and SN74HC163N: presettable 4-bit synchronous binary counters with synchronous load, two count enables and a ripple-carry output, in the 16-pin plastic DIP (TI ordering table: "PDIP – N"). Suggested display name `SN74HC161 / SN74HC163 (DIP-16)`, the same in all locales.

One item or two: one. TI's two data sheets draw the DIP with the same name at every one of the 16 pins (checked pin by pin on enlarged images, overbars included), and TI's two logic diagrams carry the same pin numbers. onsemi covers both parts with a single pin assignment figure. The only difference is the clear: asynchronous on the 'HC161, synchronous on the 'HC163 (TI page 2 of each sheet; onsemi page 2 "DEVICE/MODE TABLE" and page 7). It goes into the pin 1 text and the note.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC161 in D, N, NS and PW, and SN54HC161 in J and W: caption of TI's SN74HC161 pin figure ("SN54HC161 . . . J OR W PACKAGE", "SN74HC161 . . . D, N, NS, OR PW PACKAGE", "(TOP VIEW)").
- SN74HC163 in D, DB, N, NS and PW, and SN54HC163 in J and W: caption of TI's SN74HC163 pin figure.
- onsemi MC74HC161A and MC74HC163A: one Figure 1 "Pin Assignment" for both; page 1 says they "are identical in pinout to the LS161 and LS163". onsemi marks this document "DISCONTINUED", "THIS DEVICE IS NOT RECOMMENDED FOR NEW DESIGN".
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC161 and SN54HC163.
- 74HCT161 / 74HCT163: not stated in the documents read, so not claimed.

Suggested item note (TI page 2 of each sheet and the SN74HC161 timing diagram on page 5; onsemi page 7): the counter counts only while ENP, ENT, ~LOAD and ~CLR are all high; each rising edge at CLK then adds one. QA is the lowest bit. A low at ~LOAD loads A to D at the next rising edge. The SN74HC161 clears as soon as ~CLR goes low; the SN74HC163 clears only at the next rising clock edge, so a gate that decodes the outputs can shorten the count. Otherwise the two parts are pin-compatible. / Der Zähler zählt nur, wenn ENP, ENT, ~LOAD und ~CLR alle high sind; dann erhöht jede steigende Flanke an CLK den Zählerstand um eins. QA ist das niedrigste Bit. Ein low an ~LOAD übernimmt A bis D mit der nächsten steigenden Flanke. Der SN74HC161 löscht sofort, wenn ~CLR low wird; der SN74HC163 löscht erst mit der nächsten steigenden Taktflanke, so dass ein Gatter an den Ausgängen die Zählfolge verkürzen kann. Sonst sind beide Bausteine pinkompatibel.

## View
Shape `dip`, 16 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's page 1 figure of each sheet is captioned with the J/W and D/(DB)/N/NS/PW packages and "(TOP VIEW)". It draws the notch at the top, 1–8 down the left (CLR, CLK, A, B, C, D, ENP, GND) and 16–9 down the right (VCC, RCO, QA, QB, QC, QD, ENT, LOAD), with overbars on CLR and LOAD. I rendered page 1 of both sheets to images, enlarged both DIP figures three times and compared them label by label. onsemi's Figure 1 "Pin Assignment" draws the package upright with a dot at pin 1 and the same pin order.

## Cross-check
TI 'HC161 against TI 'HC163: same name at all 16 pins in the DIP figures, same pin numbers in the two logic diagrams. TI against onsemi: all 16 pins agree (clear 1, clock 2, preset inputs 3 to 6 with the lowest bit on 3, enable P 7, GND 8, load 9, enable T 10, outputs 11 to 14 with the highest bit on 11, carry 15, VCC 16). Inside each TI sheet the pin figure and the logic diagram agree; inside onsemi's sheet Figure 1, Figure 2 (with pin numbers, "PIN 16 = VCC", "PIN 8 = GND") and the page 7 pin descriptions agree.

Bit order: onsemi page 7 says "P0 (Pin 3) is the least-significant bit and P3 (Pin 6) is the most-significant bit" and "Q0 (Pin 14) is the least-significant bit and Q3 (Pin 11) is the most-significant bit". TI's timing diagram presets "binary 12" with C and D high and A and B low, which gives the same order.

Clear: TI 'HC161 "A low level at the clear (CLR) input sets all four of the flip-flop outputs low, regardless of the levels of the CLK, load (LOAD), or enable inputs"; TI 'HC163 "sets all four of the flip-flop outputs low after the next low-to-high transition of CLK". onsemi: "The HC161A resets asynchronously, and the HC163A resets with the rising edge of the Clock input".

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Names: TI `CLR`, `LOAD` (overbars), `A` to `D`, `QA` to `QD`, `ENP`, `ENT`, `RCO`; onsemi `RESET`, `LOAD` (no overbar in the pin assignment, inversion circles in the logic diagram), `P0` to `P3`, `Q0` to `Q3`, `ENABLE P`, `ENABLE T`, `RIPPLE CARRY OUT`.
- RCO: TI's text says RCO gives "a high-level pulse while the count is maximum (9 or 15 with QA high)", text shared with the decade counters; for these binary parts onsemi gives the maximum as 1111 and "Ripple Carry Out = Enable T · Q0 · Q1 · Q2 · Q3". The pin text says count 15.
- Packages: onsemi lists SOIC-16 and TSSOP-16, no DIP. TI's 'HC161 figure caption has no DB; the 'HC163 caption has one.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +7.0 V. Recommended 2 V to 6 V in both.

## Choices
- One item, `sn74hc161`, for both parts (see Part). If the catalog wants a separate `sn74hc163` entry later, its pin table is this one with the pin 1 text cut to the synchronous case.
- Names follow TI. Overbars: TI prints `CLR` and `LOAD` with an overbar; this record writes `~CLR` and `~LOAD`. If the catalog strips overbars as in the shipped `sn74hc138` item, write `CLR` and `LOAD`; the function text already says "active low". No name collision arises here.
- TI prints `QA` to `QD` with subscript letters; the names are written `QA` to `QD`.
- Types: A to D and QA to QD are `data`: TI's timing diagram labels them "Data Inputs" and "Data Outputs", and onsemi "PRESET DATA INPUTS". The clock, enables, load, clear and RCO are `signal`. CLK stays `signal` like the shipped `cd4017` CLOCK, because counting is its main job; typing it `data` like the shipped `sn74hc165` CLK would also be defensible.
- Pin 16 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V.
- "Otherwise the two parts are pin-compatible" in the note rests on the identical TI figures and onsemi's single figure for both.
- TI's old-format sheets have no pin function table; the pin texts are written from TI's description on page 2, the timing diagram and onsemi's page 7.
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
- Catalog names: the overbars are dropped (`CLR`, `LOAD`) with "active low" in the function text, as in `sn74hc138`.
