# l293d — L293D quadruple half-H driver (DIP-16) / L293D vierfacher Halbbrückentreiber (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker document (Texas Instruments). Part: L293D quadruple high-current half-H driver with output clamp diodes, in the 16-pin PDIP (TI package NE, L293DNE), 16 pins. The same document and the same figure cover the L293 (L293NE) without the clamp diodes (TI Figures 4 and 5 show the diodes only for the L293D). Second maker NOT read (see UNVERIFIED).
Pins: 16; types: power 2, ground 4, signal 10

Suggested pin note for 4, 5, 12 and 13 (read, TI Pin Functions table): "Connect all four to the ground plane with several solid vias: they also carry the heat away." / "Alle vier mit mehreren massiven Durchkontaktierungen an die Massefläche anschließen: Sie führen auch die Wärme ab."

Suggested item note (read, TI page 1, Pin Functions and Table 1): two separate supplies: VCC1 (pin 16) feeds the logic, VCC2 (pin 8) feeds the outputs and must be at least VCC1. Each output follows its A input while its enable pin is high and is high impedance while the enable is low. The L293D has the clamp diodes for inductive loads built in; the L293 in the same figure has not. Up to 600 mA per channel (1.2 A peak) for the L293D. / Zwei getrennte Versorgungen: VCC1 (Pin 16) speist die Logik, VCC2 (Pin 8) die Ausgänge und muss mindestens VCC1 betragen. Jeder Ausgang folgt seinem A-Eingang, solange der zugehörige Enable-Pin high ist, und ist hochohmig, solange er low ist. Der L293D hat die Freilaufdioden für induktive Lasten eingebaut, der L293 derselben Zeichnung nicht. Bis 600 mA je Kanal (1,2 A Spitze) beim L293D.

## Choices
- Catalog item: all four centre pins are `GROUND` (TI's table name) typed `ground`, the function text says "Ground and heat sink" as TI's figure does; VCC1 is the logic supply and VCC2 the motor supply with TI's section 6.3 ranges; the ground pins carry the heat-sink note.
- `GROUND` is TI's table name for all four centre pins; the figure says "HEAT SINK AND GROUND". All four are `ground`.
- Supplies, read in TI section 6.3: VCC1 4.5 V to 7 V; VCC2 from VCC1 to 36 V. The Pin Functions table describes VCC1 as "5-V supply for internal logic translation" and VCC2 as "Power VCC for drivers 4.5 V to 36 V". The pin texts above use the 6.3 ranges. Absolute maximum (6.1): 36 V for both.
- Inputs and enables are typed `signal` (logic control pins); the outputs are `signal` too, as in the `uln2003` item.
- Current: L293D 600 mA continuous, 1.2 A peak (page 1 features; section 6.1 "Peak output current … t ≤ 100 µs: L293D" 1.2 A). L293: 1 A and 2 A. Only the L293D numbers go in the note.
- Enable behaviour: Table 1, A=H EN=H → Y=H; A=L EN=H → Y=L; EN=L → Z. Note (2): in thermal shutdown the output is high impedance.
- Overbars: none in this part.
