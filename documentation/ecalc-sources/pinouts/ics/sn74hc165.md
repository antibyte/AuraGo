# sn74hc165 — SN74HC165 parallel-in shift register (DIP-16) / SN74HC165 Schieberegister mit parallelen Eingängen (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 2, data 12

## Part
SN74HC165N: 8-bit parallel-load shift register with serial output, complementary serial output, serial input and gated clock, in the 16-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (16)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC165 (DIP-16)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC165 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC165 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's figure and the 16-pin column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC165A and MC74HCT165A: one pin assignment figure for both. Page 1 says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS165".
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC165.

Suggested item note (TI page 1, section 8.1 and Table 1): the parallel inputs are split, A to D on pins 11 to 14 and E to H on pins 3 to 6. A low SH/~LD loads all eight inputs at once, whatever the clock does. With SH/~LD high and CLK INH low, each rising edge of CLK shifts one step: H comes out at QH first, and SER moves into the first stage. CLK and CLK INH are interchangeable; change CLK INH to high only while CLK is high. / Die Paralleleingänge sind geteilt: A bis D an den Pins 11 bis 14, E bis H an den Pins 3 bis 6. Ein low an SH/~LD lädt alle acht Eingänge auf einmal, unabhängig vom Takt. Bei SH/~LD high und CLK INH low schiebt jede steigende Flanke an CLK um eine Stufe: H erscheint zuerst an QH, und SER rückt in die erste Stufe nach. CLK und CLK INH sind vertauschbar; CLK INH nur auf high schalten, während CLK high ist.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 16 pins: same number for the shift/load input (1), the clock (2), E to H (3 to 6), the complementary output (7), GND (8), the serial output (9), the serial input (10), A to D (11 to 14), the clock inhibit (15) and VCC (16). The two function tables agree: low on pin 1 loads in parallel, a rising edge on pin 2 with pin 15 low shifts, and the two clock pins are interchangeable. Inside TI's document the figure, the Pin Functions table and the logic diagram on page 1 agree on all 16 pins; inside onsemi's document Figure 1 and Figure 2 agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Pin 1: TI `SH/LD` with an overbar on LD; onsemi "SERIAL SHIFT/PARALLEL LOAD" with an overbar on PARALLEL LOAD.
- Pin 2 and 15: TI `CLK` and `CLK INH`; onsemi `CLOCK` and `CLOCK INHIBIT`.
- Pin 10: TI `SER`; onsemi `SA`.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-16, TSSOP-16 and QFN16, no DIP. It confirms the 16-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names: pin 1 is `SH/LD` (overbar dropped, "active low" for LD is in the function text and item note), pin 7 stays `~QH` because it must differ from pin 9 `QH`.
- Names follow TI, the primary source.
- Overbars: TI prints an overbar on `LD` in `SH/LD` (pin 1) and on `QH` at pin 7. This record writes `SH/~LD` and `~QH`. The existing `sn74hc595` item uses another convention: it drops the overbar and writes the bare name (`OE`, `SRCLR`), with "active low" in the function text. That convention cannot be applied to pin 7 here: without a mark, pin 7 and pin 9 would both be called `QH`. Pin 1 could be written `SH/LD` under the 595 convention; pin 7 needs a mark in any case. Do not use `QH'` for it: the `sn74hc595` item already uses `QH'` for a different thing (its serial carry output).
- `CLK INH` keeps TI's space.
- Types follow the `sn74hc595` record: serial data, parallel data and the shift clock are `data` (A to H, SER, QH, ~QH, CLK); the mode and gating controls are `signal` (SH/~LD, CLK INH). CLK INH is electrically a second clock input (TI: "The functions of CLK and CLK INH are interchangeable"); it is typed `signal` because its name and normal use are a control. Typing it `data` like CLK would also be defensible.
- "H comes out at QH first" is read from TI's Figure 4 (typical shift, load and inhibit sequence) and from the logic diagram, where H is the stage that drives QH.
- Pin 16 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3).
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
