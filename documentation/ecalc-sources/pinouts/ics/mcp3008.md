# mcp3008 — MCP3008 8-channel 10-bit ADC with SPI (DIP-16) / MCP3008 8-Kanal-10-Bit-A/D-Wandler mit SPI (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker document (Microchip, the only maker; no second maker exists for this part number). Part: MCP3008 10-bit successive-approximation A/D converter with 8 single-ended (or 4 pseudo-differential) inputs and an SPI interface, in the 16-pin PDIP, 16 pins. Microchip's figure and table give the same numbers for the PDIP and the SOIC. The MCP3004 of the same document is NOT pin compatible: it is a 14-pin part (4 channels, NC on 5 and 6, DGND on 7).
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 2, signal 9, data 4

## Pins
Names are Microchip's Table 3-1 symbols. Microchip prints an overbar on `CS` in `CS/SHDN` (pin 10) and subscripts in `DIN`, `DOUT`, `VREF`, `VDD`; the names here drop the overbar and the subscripts, and the function says active low.

Suggested item note (read, Microchip sections 3.3, 3.7, 6.4 and the electrical characteristics): each analog input may swing from VSS to VREF; VREF itself may be 0.25 V to VDD. Pull CS/SHDN high between conversions. Use a 1 µF bypass capacitor close to the chip, and tie both AGND and DGND to the analog ground plane (to VSS if there is no plane). Pairs of channels can also be used as pseudo-differential inputs. / Jeder Analogeingang darf zwischen VSS und VREF liegen; VREF selbst darf 0,25 V bis VDD betragen. CS/SHDN zwischen zwei Wandlungen auf high ziehen. Einen 1-µF-Abblockkondensator nahe am Baustein vorsehen und AGND wie DGND an die analoge Massefläche legen (ohne Massefläche an VSS). Kanalpaare lassen sich auch als pseudo-differenzielle Eingänge nutzen.

## Choices
- Catalog item: names are Microchip's Table 3-1 symbols without overbar and subscripts (`CS/SHDN`, active low in the function text); `CS/SHDN` is typed `data` together with `CLK`, `DIN` and `DOUT`; `AGND` and `DGND` are `ground`; the MCP3004 (14 pins) is left out and named in the item notes.
- `CS/SHDN` is written without its overbar (only `CS` carries it in Microchip's print); the function says "active low". The `~` convention of the `sn74hc165` record is not needed, because no other pin would get the same name.
- Types: the SPI pins `CLK`, `DIN`, `DOUT` and the chip select `CS/SHDN` are `data` (serial bus pins as planned). `CS/SHDN` also controls standby; typing it `signal` would also be defensible. The analog inputs and `VREF` are `signal` (as `AREF` in the `atmega328p` item). `DGND` and `AGND` are `ground`.
- Supply: Table 3-1 "+2.7V to 5.5V Power Supply" and the operating voltage line of the electrical characteristics.
- `VREF` range 0.25 V to VDD and the input range VSS to VREF are from the electrical characteristics (page 3). Absolute maximum: VDD 7.0 V, all inputs and outputs −0.6 V to VDD + 0.6 V (page 3).
- "Pull CS/SHDN high between conversions": section 3.7, "The CS/SHDN pin must be pulled high between conversions."
- The MCP3004 (14 pins) is left out; it is not pin compatible.
