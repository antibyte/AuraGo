# lm324 — LM324 quad op-amp (DIP-14) / LM324 Vierfach-OPV (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 14; types: power 2, signal 12

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 5-1 is captioned for the 14-pin packages including PDIP (package N) and "(Top View)". It marks pin 1 with a dot at the top left and runs 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at it.

## Sources
1. Texas Instruments, LM124, LM224, LM324, LM2902 families, Quadruple Operational Amplifiers, SLOS066AE (August 1975, revised September 2025), section 5, Figure 5-1 and Table 5-1. https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm324.pdf — covers LM324 in N (PDIP, 14), read PDF page 3, accessed 2026-11-04.

## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure and Table 5-1 agree on all 14 pins. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED.

## Choices
- `VCC-` is typed `power`, not `ground`, for the same reason as `V-` of the LM358: TI's table calls it the negative (lowest) supply, or ground in single-supply use.
- Pin names use ASCII minus (`1IN-`, `VCC-`) where TI prints an en dash.
- The item's notes point out that the supply pins sit in the middle of the package (4 and 11), a usual wiring slip.
- The 20-pin FK and the WQFN-16 packages are left out.
