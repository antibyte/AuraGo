# sn74hc138 — SN74HC138 3-to-8 line decoder (DIP-16) / SN74HC138 3-zu-8-Decoder (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 14

## Part
SN74HC138N: 3-line to 8-line decoder/demultiplexer with three enable inputs and eight active-low outputs, in the 16-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (16)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC138 (DIP-16)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC138 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC138 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's figure and the 16-pin column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC138A and MC74HCT138A: one pin assignment figure for both. The same page says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS138".
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC138.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 16 pins: same number for the three select inputs, the three enables with their polarity, Y0 to Y7, GND (8) and VCC (16). The two function tables agree too: enable needs pin 6 high and pins 4 and 5 low, and the selected output is low. Inside TI's document the figure and the Pin Functions table agree on all 16 pins; inside onsemi's document the pin assignment and the logic diagram agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Select inputs: TI `A`, `B`, `C`; onsemi `A0`, `A1`, `A2`. Same order: pin 1 is the least significant bit in both.
- Enables: TI `G2A` and `G2B` with overbars and `G1`; onsemi `CS2`, `CS3` and `CS1`. onsemi's pin assignment prints no overbar; its logic diagram shows inversion circles on CS2 (pin 4) and CS3 (pin 5), and its function table gives the same polarity as TI.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-16, TSSOP-16 and QFN16, no DIP. It confirms the 16-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names drop the overbar, as the `sn74hc595` item does: pins 4 and 5 are `G2A` and `G2B`, and the function text says "active low".
- Names follow TI, the primary source.
- Overbars: TI prints `G2A` and `G2B` with an overbar in the figure and in the table. This record writes them `~G2A` and `~G2B`. The existing `sn74hc595` item uses another convention: it drops the overbar and writes the bare name (`OE`, `SRCLR`), with "active low" in the function text. If the catalog should stay with that convention, strip the `~` (`G2A`, `G2B`); the function text already says "active low" either way.
- The outputs are active low, but TI gives `Y0` to `Y7` no overbar, so they get no `~`. The function text says "low when selected".
- All logic pins are `signal`, select inputs included: they are address lines of a decoder, not a data bus.
- Pin 16 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3).
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
