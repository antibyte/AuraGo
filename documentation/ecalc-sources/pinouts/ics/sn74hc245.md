# sn74hc245 — SN74HC245 octal bus transceiver (DIP-20) / SN74HC245 achtfach Bus-Transceiver (DIP-20)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 20; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 2, data 16

## Part
SN74HC245N: eight non-inverting bidirectional bus drivers with 3-state outputs, one direction input and one output-enable input, in the 20-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP, 20"). Suggested display name `SN74HC245 (DIP-20)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC245 in SSOP (DB), TVSOP (DGV), SOIC (DW) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC245 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's Figure 5-1 and the pin table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC245A and MC74HCT245A: one pin assignment figure for both. Page 1 says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS245".
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC245. It also has 20 pins, but in another order (TI Figure 5-2).

Suggested item note (TI Figure 5-1, Table 8-1 and section 8.1): the B side is shifted by one row against the A side: A1 (pin 2) faces ~OE (pin 19), A2 (pin 3) faces B1 (pin 18), and B8 (pin 11) faces GND (pin 10). With ~OE low, DIR high drives the B side from the A side and DIR low drives the A side from the B side; with ~OE high both sides are high-impedance. TI advises a pull-up resistor from ~OE to VCC so that the outputs stay off during power-up. / Die B-Seite ist gegenüber der A-Seite um eine Reihe versetzt: A1 (Pin 2) liegt gegenüber von ~OE (Pin 19), A2 (Pin 3) gegenüber von B1 (Pin 18) und B8 (Pin 11) gegenüber von GND (Pin 10). Bei ~OE low treibt DIR high die B-Seite von der A-Seite, DIR low die A-Seite von der B-Seite; bei ~OE high sind beide Seiten hochohmig. TI empfiehlt einen Pull-up-Widerstand von ~OE nach VCC, damit die Ausgänge beim Einschalten abgeschaltet bleiben.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 20 pins: direction on 1, A1 to A8 on 2 to 9, GND on 10, B8 down to B1 on 11 to 18, output enable on 19, VCC on 20. The two function tables agree word for word in meaning: enable low and direction low sends B to A; enable low and direction high sends A to B; enable high isolates the buses. Inside TI's document Figure 5-1 and the pin table agree on all 20 pins; inside onsemi's document the pin assignment and the logic diagram agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Pin 1: TI `DIR`; onsemi `DIRECTION`.
- Pin 19: TI `OE` with an overbar in Figure 5-1 (the table row prints it without); onsemi "OUTPUT ENABLE" without an overbar, with an inversion circle in the logic diagram and "active-low Output Enable" in the text.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-20 and TSSOP-20 (plus QFN in the ratings table), no DIP. It confirms the 20-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both.

Oddity inside TI's document, not touching the pins: the TYPE column of the pin table marks `DIR` and `OE` as "I/O", like the bus pins. Table 8-1 lists both under INPUTS and section 8.1 calls them the direction-control input and the output-enable input, so they are inputs. They are typed `signal` here.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog name of pin 19 is `OE` (overbar dropped, as in the `sn74hc595` item); the function text says "active low".
- Names follow TI, the primary source.
- Overbars: TI's Figure 5-1 prints `OE` with an overbar. This record writes `~OE`. The existing `sn74hc595` item uses another convention: it drops the overbar and writes the bare name (`OE`, `SRCLR`), with "active low" in the function text. If the catalog should stay with that convention, strip the `~` (`OE`); the function text already says "active low" either way.
- A1 to A8 and B1 to B8 are `data` (bus pins); DIR and ~OE are `signal` (controls), as in the `sn74hc595` record.
- TI's table describes the bus pins as "A1 Input/Output"; the function text says "Bus A, bit 1". TI numbers the bits 1 to 8, not 0 to 7, and the text keeps that.
- Pin 20 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3).
- The FK package is left out.
