# uln2803 — ULN2803A eight Darlington sink drivers (DIP-18) / ULN2803A acht Darlington-Lowside-Treiber (DIP-18)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments ULN2803A, cross-check STMicroelectronics ULN2801A–ULN2804A; also TI's original 1997 issue). All three were read from distributor copies of the makers' own PDFs, because TI has withdrawn the ULN2803A datasheet from ti.com and st.com did not answer (see Sources).
Pins: 18; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 16

## Part
ULN2803AN: eight NPN Darlington sink drivers with common emitters and common-cathode clamp diodes, 2.7 kΩ series base resistor per channel for TTL and 5 V CMOS, in the 18-pin plastic DIP (TI "N", "PDIP (18)"; ST "DIP-18"). Display name `ULN2803A (DIP-18)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- ULN2803A in the 18-pin SOIC (DW): TI's figure is captioned "DW OR N PACKAGE (TOP VIEW)".
- ST ULN2801A, ULN2802A, ULN2803A and ULN2804A (DIP-18): one "Pin connections (top view)" figure for all four; they differ in the input stage (ULN2802A 10.5 kΩ and Zener for 14–25 V PMOS, ULN2804A 10.5 kΩ for 6–15 V CMOS).
- onsemi ULN2804A (from the earlier onsemi read, one figure with the ULN2803).
- Not this pinout: the TI ULN2803C, which TI sells as the successor, comes only in the 20-pin SOIC (DW) (TI SLRS076). Not claimed: Toshiba TBD62083 (different part, not read).
- TI page 1 says "Compatible with ULN2800A Series"; this is a feature line, not a pin statement, and is not used as one.

## View
Shape `dip`, 18 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI SLRS049G section 6, "DW OR N PACKAGE (TOP VIEW)", shows a notch at the top, 1B–8B and GND on pins 1–9 down the left and 1C–8C and COM on pins 18–10 down the right; the 1997 issue SLRS049 draws the same figure as "N PACKAGE (TOP VIEW)". ST's Figure 2 "Pin connections (top view)" shows the same numbers (no notch drawn), with an inverter symbol from each input to the output straight across and a diode from each output to pin 10. I rendered TI PDF page 3 (2015), TI page 1 (1997) and ST page 4 to images and looked at the three figures.

## Cross-check
TI (2015 figure), TI (1997 figure) and ST agree pin by pin on all 18 pins: inputs 1–8 (channel n on pin n), GND 9, diode common 10, outputs 11–18 (channel n on pin 19 − n). They also agree with the onsemi ULN2803/D drawing read in the earlier batch.

Contradiction inside TI SLRS049G: its "Pin Functions" table (page 3) gives GND as pin 7 and COM as pin 8 and says "Channel 1 through 7", which is the ULN2003A's table; it disagrees with TI's own figure on the same page, with the 1997 figure, with ST and with onsemi, which all put GND on 9 and COM on 10 with eight channels. The figure is followed; the table is not used for pins 9 and 10.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Names: TI writes `1B` … `8B`, `1C` … `8C`, `GND`, `COM`; ST writes `IN 1` … `IN 8`, `OUT 1` … `OUT 8`, `GND` and labels pin 10 "COMMON FREE WHEELING DIODES".
- Ratings: TI 7.3 recommends input 0 V to 5 V and output 0 V to 50 V; ST lists 50 V output, 30 V input and 500 mA as absolute maxima, −20 °C to 85 °C ambient.

## Choices
- Names follow TI's figure, matching the shipped `uln2003` item (`1B`, `1C`, `COM`). Pin 9 is `GND` here (TI's name), where the ULN2003A item has `E`.
- Types follow the `uln2003` item: inputs and outputs `signal`, pin 9 `ground`, pin 10 (COM) `power`.
- "3.3 V" in the note rests on TI 9.1 ("TTL or CMOS operating at supply voltages of 5.0 V or 3.3 V") and Figure 13 (3.3 V logic); TI page 1 itself only says "TTL or 5-V CMOS".
- The comparison with the ULN2003A (ground and COM on pins 8 and 9 there) is read from the shipped `uln2003` item.
