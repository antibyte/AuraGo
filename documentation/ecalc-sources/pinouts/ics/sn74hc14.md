# sn74hc14 — SN74HC14 hex Schmitt-trigger inverter (DIP-14) / SN74HC14 sechsfach Schmitt-Trigger-Inverter (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
SN74HC14N: six independent inverters with Schmitt-trigger inputs in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP, 14"). Suggested display name `SN74HC14 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC14 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SOP (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC14 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's Figure 4-1 and the 14-pin column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC14A and MC74HCT14A: one pin assignment figure for both.
- The numbers are the same as in the `sn74hc04` record (my own comparison of the two TI tables, not a statement of either datasheet).
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC14.

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's "Figure 4-1. D, DB, N, NS, PW, J, or W Package 14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP, CDIP, or CFP Top View" shows 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right. This figure draws neither a notch nor a pin 1 dot; the orientation follows from the numbers and the caption. onsemi's pin assignment "14-Lead (Top View)" shows the notch at the pin 1 / pin 14 end, which confirms it. I rendered TI PDF page 3 and onsemi page 1 to images and looked at both figures.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins. Inside TI's document Figure 4-1, the "Functional pinout" on page 1 and the Pin Functions table agree; inside onsemi's document the logic diagram (with pin numbers) and the pin assignment agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Naming order: TI `1A`, `1Y`; onsemi `A1`, `Y1`.
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP. It confirms the 14-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V; onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range 2 V to 6 V in both.

Oddity inside TI's document, not touching the pins: the Device Information table on page 1 of SCLS085L prints the part numbers as "SN74AHC14" and "SN54AHC14". The header, the title, the product links and every other page say SN74HC14 and SN54HC14, so this looks like an editing slip. I report it in case a reader compares the table; the package list in that table (N = PDIP, 14) is the one used here.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names are TI's, as in the table above (`1A`, `1Y`).
- Names follow TI (`1A`, `1Y`), the primary source.
- No pin carries an overbar. For the record: the existing `sn74hc595` item drops overbars and writes bare names (`OE`, `SRCLR`) with "active low" in the function text; the other records of this batch mark an overbar with a leading `~`.
- All inverter pins are `signal`.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 5.3).
- TI's table says "Channel 1, Input A"; the function text says "Inverter 1, Schmitt-trigger input", so that the page differs visibly from the plain inverter 74HC04.
- The 20-pin FK package is left out.
