# lm339 — LM339 quad comparator (DIP-14) / LM339 Vierfachkomparator (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker document, cross-checked against a second maker. Part: LM339 quad differential comparator in the 14-pin PDIP (TI package N), 14 pins. Pin-compatible relatives read in the same documents: TI draws one figure (Figure 5-1) for the D, DB, N, NS and PW packages of all devices on the sheet (LM139, LM239, LM339, LM339A, LM339B, LM2901, LM2901B, LM2901V, LM2901AV); its Device Information lists PDIP (14) for "LM239, LM339x, LM2901". TI page 1: the LM339B and LM2901B "can drop-in replace the LM239, LM339 and LM2901". onsemi's sheet covers LM339, LM339E, LM239, LM2901, LM2901E, LM2901V, NCV2901 and MC3302 with one pin drawing.
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Pins
Names are the ones printed in TI's Figure 5-1 (DIP drawing). TI prints an en dash in `2IN–`; the name here uses the ASCII minus, as the existing `lm324` and `lm393` items do. The function text names the pins of the same comparator, because TI's own table and onsemi number the channels 1 and 2 the other way round (see Cross-check).

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 5-1 is captioned "D, DB, N, NS, PW Packages", "14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SOP, TSSOP" and "Top View". It marks pin 1 with a circle in the top-left corner (no notch drawn), and shows 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right. The internal lines in the figure join pin 1 to the comparator whose inputs are pins 6 and 7, pin 2 to the one on pins 4 and 5, pin 13 to the one on pins 10 and 11, pin 14 to the one on pins 8 and 9. I rendered PDF page 4 to an image (full page and a 5000-pixel-wide crop of the figure) and looked at both. onsemi's figure "PIN CONNECTIONS" "(Top View)" on its page 1 shows a notch on top, 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right, with the same internal joins; I rendered that page and looked at a crop of the figure.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree on the position and role of every pin: same supply pins (3 VCC, 12 GND), outputs on 1, 2, 13 and 14, inverting inputs on 4, 6, 8 and 10, non-inverting inputs on 5, 7, 9 and 11, and the same pairing of each output with its two inputs (checked in both drawings).

Differences, names only:
- Channels 1 and 2 are numbered the other way round. TI Figure 5-1: pin 1 `1OUT`, pin 2 `2OUT`, pins 4/5 `2IN–`/`2IN+`, pins 6/7 `1IN–`/`1IN+`. onsemi: pin 1 "Output 2", pin 2 "Output 1", pins 4/5 "– Input 1"/"+ Input 1", pins 6/7 "– Input 2"/"+ Input 2". TI's Table 5-1 footnote (1) says so: "Some manufacturers transpose the names of channels 1 & 2. Electrically the pinouts are identical".
- Inside TI's own document, Table 5-1 is not consistent with Figure 5-1 in its wording: the NAME column uses `OUT1`, `IN2–` style (pin 1 `OUT1`, pin 4 `IN2–`, pin 6 `IN1–`, matching the figure's channel numbers), but the DESCRIPTION column says pin 1 "Output pin of the comparator 2", pin 2 "Output pin of the comparator 1", pins 4/5 "… of the comparator 1" and pins 6/7 "… of the comparator 2". The pin numbers in the table and the figure agree; only the channel labels in the descriptions are swapped. Pins 3 and 8–14 agree everywhere.
- Pin 3: TI `VCC`, onsemi `VCC`. Pin 12: TI `GND` (table description "Negative supply"), onsemi `GND`.
- Packages: onsemi's sheet lists SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP. It confirms the 14-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Supply range: TI section 6.5, non-B non-V devices (plain LM339): 2 V to 30 V. onsemi feature list: single supply 3.0 V to 36 V, split supply ±1.5 V to ±18 V. The makers differ.

## Choices
- Catalog item: names follow TI's Figure 5-1 (`1OUT`, `2IN-` ...); each output's and input's function text names the pins of its comparator (output of the comparator with the inputs on pins 6 and 7, ...) because the makers, and TI's own table against its figure, number comparators 1 and 2 the other way round; the pull-up note sits on pins 1, 2, 13 and 14.
- Names follow TI's Figure 5-1 (`1OUT`, `2IN-` …), the same style as the existing `lm324` and the `lm393` record. Table 5-1's `OUT1`/`IN1–` style is the same information in another order of letters.
- Because the channel numbering of comparators 1 and 2 differs between makers (and inside TI's table), each output's function text names the input pins of its comparator. That keeps the page right whatever label a reader's own datasheet uses.
- `GND` is typed `ground`, as in the `lm393` record: TI names the pin GND. TI's table describes it as "Negative supply"; the function says "Ground (negative supply)". With onsemi's split supply, pin 12 is the negative rail.
- Open collector, read in TI: section 7.1 says the open-collector outputs allow level shifting independent of VCC and an AND function when outputs are tied together; section 7.3 says the output is an NPN pull-down that sinks current when the negative input is higher than the positive input; section 7.4.1 says the output is "logic low or high impedance (logic high with pullup)". So a pull-up is needed for a high level, and the output is low when IN− is above IN+. TI writes "open drain" in sections 7.3 and 8.1 for the same NPN output; the record says open collector, as section 7.1 and the `lm393` record do.
- Supply pin text says only "Positive supply", because the makers' ranges differ. If a range is wanted, TI's 2 V to 30 V for the plain LM339 is the narrower one.
- TI section 6.2 note (4) and onsemi Maximum Ratings note 1: a short from an output to VCC can cause excessive heating and eventual destruction. Worth a line in the item notes.
- Left out: the 16-pad WQFN (RTE) package of Figure 5-2 with its NC pads and thermal pad, and the SOT-23 (14) package.
