# lm386 — LM386 audio power amplifier (DIP-8) / LM386 NF-Leistungsverstärker (DIP-8)

Kind: authoritative maker document (Texas Instruments, which took over National Semiconductor, the original maker). Part: LM386 low-voltage audio power amplifier in the 8-pin PDIP (TI package P: LM386N-1, LM386N-3, LM386N-4), 8 pins. Same pin table in the same document for the SOIC (LM386M-1, LM386MX-1) and VSSOP (LM386MMX-1). No second maker read (see UNVERIFIED).
Pins: 8; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 6

## Pins
Names are the ones in TI's Table 5-1 (figure and table agree). TI writes `–INPUT` and `+INPUT` with an en dash in the table and "- INPUT" / "+ INPUT" with a space in the figure; `VOUT` and `VS` are printed with subscripts. The names here use the ASCII minus, no space and no subscript.

Suggested item note (read, TI sections 8.1, 9.2.1.2.1 and 9.2.1.2.2): with pins 1 and 8 open the gain is 20; a capacitor from pin 1 to pin 8 bypasses the internal 1.35 kΩ resistor and gives 200; a resistor in series with that capacitor sets any gain between. Both inputs are biased to ground through 50 kΩ inside. At high gain the unused input must be bypassed to ground (0.1 µF or a short). / Mit offenen Pins 1 und 8 ist die Verstärkung 20; ein Kondensator von Pin 1 nach Pin 8 überbrückt den internen 1,35-kΩ-Widerstand und ergibt 200; ein Widerstand in Reihe zum Kondensator stellt jeden Wert dazwischen ein. Beide Eingänge liegen intern über 50 kΩ an Masse. Bei hoher Verstärkung muss der unbenutzte Eingang nach Masse abgeblockt werden (0,1 µF oder Kurzschluss).

## View
Shape `dip`, 8 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 5-1 shows a notch on top, 1–4 down the left and 8–5 down the right, captioned "Top View". Caveat: the caption reads "D Package 8-Pin MSOP Top View", that is, it names only one package (and mixes the SOIC letter D with the word MSOP); it does not name the PDIP. What ties the drawing to the PDIP: (a) there is only this one pin figure and one pin table in the document, while section 8.1 says the device "comes in three different 8-pin packages as PDIP, SOIC and VSSOP" and the Device Information lists LM386N-1/-3/-4 as PDIP (8); (b) Figure 11-1, "Layout Example for Minimum Parts Gain = 20 dB on PDIP package", shows the PDIP with its pin 1 dot: the second and fourth pins of the pin-1 row go to the ground plane (pins 2 and 4), the third gets the input trace (pin 3), and on the other row pin 5 goes to the output capacitor and Zobel network and pin 6 to the 5 V via, while pins 1, 7 and 8 are left open. That matches the table. I rendered PDF pages 3, 9 and 18 to images and looked at them.

## Choices
- Catalog item: names are TI's Table 5-1 with ASCII minus (`-INPUT`, `+INPUT`); pin 6 says only "Positive supply" because the range depends on the suffix, the ranges are in the item notes; the basis of the DIP assignment (pin table plus Figure 11-1, figure caption names only the MSOP/SOIC) is stated in the item notes as well as in View above.
- `-INPUT` / `+INPUT`: TI's table names with the en dash replaced by an ASCII minus, as the `lm358` and `lm393` names do.
- Types: TI's Table 5-1 types BYPASS as "O" and GAIN as "–" (neither input nor output). Here all of them are `signal`; GND is `ground` (TI: "Ground reference", type P) and VS is `power` (TI: "Power supply voltage", type P).
- Supply range, read in TI section 6.3: 4 V to 12 V; LM386N-4 5 V to 18 V. Absolute maximum (6.1): 15 V for LM386N-1/-3 and LM386M-1, 22 V for LM386N-4. Because it depends on the suffix, the pin 6 function says only "Positive supply"; the item notes could give "4 V to 12 V (LM386N-4: 5 V to 18 V)".
- Inputs: absolute maximum and recommended input voltage −0.4 V to +0.4 V (sections 6.1 and 6.3). Worth a line in the item notes, because a signal above ±0.4 V must not reach pins 2 and 3 directly.
- "Sits at half the supply" for pin 5: page 1, "the output automatically biases to one-half the supply voltage". "Through a capacitor" is what TI's application figures show (250 µF in Figure 11-1); TI does not state it as a rule in the text read.
- Pin 7 function: TI says only "Bypass decoupling path". In TI's section 9.2 application figures a capacitor on BYPASS is drawn; the supply rejection spec uses CBYPASS = 10 µF (electrical characteristics). The function text stays close to TI's wording.

UNVERIFIED: No second maker read. The LM386 is also sold by others (for example Nisshinbo/JRC NJM386), but the NJM386B is listed as discontinued on Nisshinbo's site and no maker-hosted datasheet was found; only aggregator copies, which do not count. TI's pin figure caption names "D Package 8-Pin MSOP" only, not the PDIP; the PDIP assignment rests on the single pin table plus the PDIP layout example (Figure 11-1), which confirms pins 2 to 6 but cannot tell pins 1, 7 and 8 apart (all three are open there). An older National Semiconductor revision with a "Dual-In-Line" caption was not available: the ti.com URL for SNAS545C served revision D.
