# sn74hc02 — SN74HC02 quad 2-input NOR gate (DIP-14) / SN74HC02 vierfach NOR-Gatter mit 2 Eingängen (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
SN74HC02N: four independent 2-input NOR gates in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (14)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC02 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC02 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC02 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's Figure 5-1 and the D/DB/N/NS/PW/J/W column of the Pin Functions table are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC02A: same numbering, and its page 1 says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS02".
- 74HCT02: not stated in either document, so not claimed.
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC02.

Suggested item note (read from the table above and from the sn74hc00 record): gates 1 and 2 run Y, A, B by rising pin number: 1Y is pin 1 (1A 2, 1B 3) and 2Y is pin 4 (2A 5, 2B 6). Gates 3 and 4 run A, B, Y: 3Y is pin 10 (3A 8, 3B 9) and 4Y is pin 13 (4A 11, 4B 12). Read from the top of the package, every gate starts with its output on both sides. This is not the 74HC00 order: the 74HC00's output pins 3, 6, 8 and 11 are inputs here. / Bei Gatter 1 und 2 folgen Y, A, B mit steigender Pin-Nummer: 1Y ist Pin 1 (1A 2, 1B 3) und 2Y ist Pin 4 (2A 5, 2B 6). Bei Gatter 3 und 4 folgen A, B, Y: 3Y ist Pin 10 (3A 8, 3B 9) und 4Y ist Pin 13 (4A 11, 4B 12). Von oben gelesen beginnt auf beiden Seiten jedes Gatter mit seinem Ausgang. Das ist nicht die Reihenfolge des 74HC00: Dessen Ausgangspins 3, 6, 8 und 11 sind hier Eingänge.

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 5-1 is captioned "D, DB, N, NS, PW, J, or W Package", "14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP, CDIP, or CFP" and "Top View". It shows 1–7 down the left (1Y, 1A, 1B, 2Y, 2A, 2B, GND) and 14–8 down the right (VCC, 4Y, 4B, 4A, 3Y, 3B, 3A), with a small circle inside the body next to pin 1 as the pin 1 mark; this figure draws no notch. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at the figure. onsemi's Figure 2 "Pin Assignment" draws the same package upright with a dot at pin 1 and the same labels.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins: 1Y on 1, 1A 2, 1B 3, 2Y 4, 2A 5, 2B 6, GND 7, 3A 8, 3B 9, 3Y 10, 4A 11, 4B 12, 4Y 13, VCC 14. Inside TI's document Figure 5-1 and the Pin Functions table agree on all 14 pins. Inside onsemi's document Figure 1 (logic diagram with pin numbers: A1 2, B1 3, Y1 1 … A4 11, B4 12, Y4 13, "PIN 14 = VCC", "PIN 7 = GND") and Figure 2 agree. Both function tables give Y high only when A and B are both low.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Naming order: TI writes gate number first (`1A`, `1Y`); onsemi writes letter first (`A1`, `Y1`).
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-14 NB and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP. It confirms the 14-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V (section 6.1); onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range is 2 V to 6 V in both.

## Choices
- Names follow TI (`1A`, `1B`, `1Y`), the primary source.
- No pin carries an overbar.
- All gate inputs and outputs are `signal`, as for the other logic gates.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3), not the absolute maximum.
- TI calls the gates "Channel 1" to "Channel 4"; the function text says "Gate", as in the shipped sn74hc00 item.
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
- The "outputs first" description in the brief holds when the package is read from the top down on each side. By pin number it holds for gates 1 and 2 only; gates 3 and 4 have the output on the highest number of the three (10 and 13). The suggested note says both.
