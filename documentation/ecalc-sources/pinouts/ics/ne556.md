# ne556 — NE556 dual timer (DIP-14) / NE556 Doppel-Timer (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check STMicroelectronics)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
NE556N: two independent bipolar timers of the NE555 type in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (14)"; ST "N DIP14 (Plastic package)"). Display name `NE556 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- NE556 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB) and SO (NS); NA556 in D and N; SA556 in N; SE556 in CDIP (J): TI's Figure 4-1 is captioned for all of them (one drawing, "Top View").
- ST NE556, SA556 and SE556 in DIP14 (N) and SO14 (D): one "Pin connections (top view)" drawing for all three.
- Not claimed: CMOS dual timers (TLC556, ICM7556) and the Fairchild LM556; no document read for them (a Fairchild LM556/NE556 sheet was downloaded but its pin drawing was not checked).

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI Figure 4-1, "(Top View)", shows 1–7 down the left (1DISCH … GND) and 14–8 down the right (VCC, 2DISCH … 2TRIG), with a small circle inside the body next to pin 1; no notch is drawn. ST's "Pin connections (top view)" on page 1 draws the same order with a notch at the top, 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right. I rendered TI PDF page 2 and ST PDF page 1 to images and looked at both figures.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins: Discharge 1/13, Threshold 2/12, Control Voltage 3/11, Reset 4/10, Output 5/9, Trigger 6/8, GND 7, VCC 14. Inside TI's document the figure and Table 4-1 agree (the table lists CONT 3, 11; DISCH 1, 13; GND 7; OUT 5, 9; RESET 4, 10; THRES 2, 12; TRIG 6, 8; VCC 14; which number belongs to which timer comes from the figure).

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Names: TI writes short names with the timer number in front (`1DISCH`, `2TRIG`); ST writes full words without a timer number (`Discharge`, `Trigger`).
- Supply: TI recommends 4.5 V to 16 V for the NE556 (18 V absolute maximum); ST's absolute maximum is also 18 V. ST's recommended range was not read.
- Packages: TI also lists SSOP (DB) and SO (NS) for the NE556; ST lists DIP14 and SO14 only.

## Choices
- Names follow TI, the primary source, and match the shipped `ne555` item (`TRIG`, `THRES`, `CONT`, `RESET`, `OUT`, `DISCH`) with the timer number in front.
- Function texts reuse the shipped `ne555` wording, with "Timer 1/2" in front and the timer's own CONT pin named.
- `RESET` carries no overbar in TI's figure; the function says "active low" (TI Table 4-1: "Active low reset input").
- Types follow the `ne555` item: GND `ground`, VCC `power`, all timer pins `signal`.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended 4.5 V to 16 V for the NE556 (5.3), not the 18 V absolute maximum. The SE556 (18 V) is a member of the same drawing but not the subject of the item.
