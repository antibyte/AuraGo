# ne555 — NE555 timer (DIP-8) / NE555 Timer (DIP-8)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 8; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 6

## Members that share this pinout
- NE555 in the SOIC (D), SO (PS) and TSSOP (PW) versions of the same sheet (the figure is captioned for them).
- TLC555 (CMOS), in its 8-pin packages (SOIC D, PDIP P, SOP PS, CDIP JG): TI's own sheet (SLFS043K, page 1) says "Functionally interchangeable with the NE555; has same pinout", and Figure 4-1 with Table 4-1 agree with the NE555 on pins 1 to 7 (GND, TRIG, OUT, RESET, CONT, THRES, DISCH). Pin 8 has the same function but is named VDD (2 V to 15 V for the TLC555C, 3 V to 15 V for the TLC555I, 5 V to 15 V for the TLC555M). Not this pinout: the TLC555 in the 14-pin TSSOP (PW; GND 1, TRIG 3, OUT 5, RESET 7, CONT 8, THRES 10, DISCH 12, VDD 14, the rest no connection) and the 20-pin LCCC (FK); the item's note says so.

## View
Shape `dip`, 8 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: the TI figure is captioned for the NE555 "D, P, PS, OR PW PACKAGE" and "(TOP VIEW)", and shows the notch on top, 1–4 down the left and 8–5 down the right. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at the figure.

## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure and Table 4-1 agree on all 8 pins. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED. The TLC555 sheet agrees pin by pin on the function of all 8 pins; its supply pin is named VDD, not VCC.

## Choices
- Name uses `DIP-8`, the same in all 16 locales: it holds only a proper name and a package name, so there is nothing to translate.
- `RESET` is written without its overbar. TI's table does not use one for this pin; the function text says active low.
- All timer pins, `OUT` included, are `signal` as planned.
- The 4.5 V to 16 V range of pin 8 is TI's table description; the SE555's 18 V is left out because this item is the NE555.
- The 20-pin FK package of the SE555 (with NC pins) is left out.
