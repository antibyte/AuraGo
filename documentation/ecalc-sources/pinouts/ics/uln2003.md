# uln2003 — ULN2003A Darlington array (DIP-16) / ULN2003A Darlington-Array (DIP-16)

Kind: authoritative maker document
Pins: 16; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 14

## View
Shape `dip`, 16 pins: package from above, notch at the top, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: TI's Figure 4-1 is captioned "D, N, NS, and PW Package 16-Pin SOIC, PDIP, SO, and TSSOP Top View". It shows the notch on top, 1–8 down the left, 16–9 down the right. ## Cross-check
One maker document is enough here. Figure and Table 4-1 agree on all 16 pins. No disagreement, nothing UNVERIFIED.

The common-cathode pin: Table 4-1 describes pin 9 (COM) as the common cathode node of the flyback diodes, required for inductive loads. Section 7.4 says to tie it to the coil supply for inductive loads and allows it to float for resistive loads. Section 8.4 says it is typically tied to the system supply and warns against outputs far above the COM voltage. All of this is in the item's notes and the pin's notes, in my own words.

## Choices
- `COM` is typed `power`, though it does not supply the chip: section 8.4 says the device needs no supply of its own and COM typically goes to the system supply, and TI's table gives it no input or output direction. A reader who meets a red pin will connect it to a rail, which is the right wiring for coils. The notes say it may stay open for resistive loads.
- `E` is typed `ground`: TI says it is typically tied to ground.
- Inputs `nB` and outputs `nC` are `signal`.
- Item name is `ULN2003A`, the part the id stands for. The datasheet also covers ULN2002A, ULN2003AI, ULQ2003A, ULN2004A and ULQ2004A in the same figure; they are not named on the page.
