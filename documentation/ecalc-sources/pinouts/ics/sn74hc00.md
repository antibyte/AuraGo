# sn74hc00 — SN74HC00 quad 2-input NAND gate (DIP-14) / SN74HC00 vierfach NAND-Gatter mit 2 Eingängen (DIP-14)

Kind: authoritative maker documents (primary Texas Instruments, cross-check onsemi)
Pins: 14; types: power 1, ground 1, signal 12

## Part
SN74HC00N: four independent 2-input NAND gates in the 14-pin plastic DIP (TI package N, "PDIP (14)"). Suggested display name `SN74HC00 (DIP-14)`, the same in all locales.

Members that share this pinout, as far as a datasheet says so:
- SN74HC00 in SOIC (D), SSOP (DB), SO (NS) and TSSOP (PW), and SN54HC00 in CDIP (J) and CFP (W): TI's figure and the 14-pin column of Table 5-1 are captioned for all of them.
- onsemi MC74HC00A: same numbering, and its page 1 says the part "is identical in pinout to the LS00".
- 74HCT00: not stated in either document, so not claimed.
- Not this pinout: the 20-pin LCCC (FK) of the SN54HC00.

## View
Shape `dip`, 14 pins: package from above, pin 1 top left, numbers counter-clockwise. Basis: the figure in TI section 5 is captioned "D, DB, N, NS, PW, J, or W Package", "14-Pin SOIC, SSOP, PDIP, SO, TSSOP, CDIP, or CFP" and "Top View". It shows 1–7 down the left and 14–8 down the right, with a small circle inside the body next to pin 1 as the pin 1 mark; this figure draws no notch. I rendered PDF page 3 to an image and looked at the figure. onsemi's Figure 2 "Pinout (Top View)" draws the same package lying on its side with the notch at the pin 1 / pin 14 end.

## Cross-check
Both sources agree pin by pin on all 14 pins: same number for every input, output, GND (7) and VCC (14). Inside TI's document the figure and Table 5-1 agree on all 14 pins. Inside onsemi's document Figure 1 (logic diagram with pin numbers) and Figure 2 agree.

Differences, none of them in the pin numbers:
- Naming order: TI writes gate number first (`1A`, `1B`, `1Y`); onsemi writes letter first (`A1`, `B1`, `Y1`).
- Packages: onsemi's document lists SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14 only, no DIP. It confirms the 14-lead numbering, not a DIP drawing as such; TI's figure covers the PDIP together with SOIC and TSSOP.
- Absolute maximum supply: TI −0.5 V to 7 V (section 6.1); onsemi −0.5 V to +6.5 V. Recommended range is 2 V to 6 V in both.

## Choices
- Catalog item: Catalog names are TI's, as in the table above (`1A`, `1B`, `1Y`).
- Names follow TI (`1A`, `1B`, `1Y`), the primary source.
- No pin of this part carries an overbar, so the active-low spelling question does not arise here. For the record: the existing `sn74hc595` item drops overbars and writes bare names (`OE`, `SRCLR`) with "active low" in the function text; the other records of this batch mark an overbar with a leading `~`.
- All gate inputs and outputs are `signal`, as planned for logic ICs.
- Pin 14 text uses TI's recommended range 2 V to 6 V (section 6.3), not the absolute maximum.
- TI calls the gates "Channel 1" to "Channel 4"; the function text says "Gate", which is the plainer word. The numbers are unchanged.
- The 20-pin FK package (with NC pins) is left out.
