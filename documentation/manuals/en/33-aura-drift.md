# Chapter 33: Aura Drift — racing on the Virtual Desktop

Aura Drift is a racing game that lives on the Virtual Desktop. It runs entirely in your
browser: no account, no provider, no network call. Race five AI opponents, chase a personal
best in time trial, drift for points in Drift Attack, or try to survive Elimination and Hot
Potato. The cars, tracks and sounds are all built for AuraGo, so nothing is downloaded.

The 3D view is the default. If your browser or graphics card cannot do 3D, the game switches
to a flat 2D view. The driving is the same either way.

## Opening and starting

Launch **Aura Drift** from the start menu under **Games**. The garage has five tabs: mode,
car, track, setup and keys. Pick a mode, a car and a track, then start. Selecting a track sets
its recommended lap count, usually two laps (about three minutes).

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys / WASD | Steer left and right, throttle up, brake or reverse down |
| Space | Fire the equipped weapon |
| Shift | Handbrake for controlled oversteer. In the air: spin |
| E or Ctrl | Nitro. E is the safer key, because Ctrl+W closes the browser tab |
| Up / Down in the air | Front flip / back flip |
| Left / Right in the air | Roll |
| C | Change camera (chase, high, drone, bumper) |
| H | Horn |
| T | Call the marshal back onto the road (not in time trial) |
| P or Escape | Pause and resume |
| R | Restart the race |

Touch devices show eight large buttons for steering, throttle, brake, fire, handbrake and
boost. Losing window focus pauses the race and releases every held key.

## Modes

| Mode | Opponents | Weapons | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quick race | 5 AI | yes | Finish position. The first finisher starts a 45-second countdown; anybody still driving when it ends is out. |
| Time trial | none | no | Your time, against your ghost (your best run, replayed). No marshal. |
| Cup | 5 AI | yes | Points across all eight tracks in a row: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8 for the top six. |
| Daily route | 5 AI | yes | A new three-lap route each day, the same for everyone. |
| Drift Attack | none | no | Style points in 90 seconds. Medals: bronze at 10,000, silver at 22,000, gold at 40,000. |
| Elimination | 5 AI | yes | Last place goes home at 45 seconds, then every 30 seconds. You get a five-second warning. |
| Hot Potato | 5 AI | air horn, shield, overdrive only | Pass the bomb on by bumping into a rival. When the fuse runs out, the holder is out and cars nearby get a shove. Each hand-over shortens the fuse, down to 10 seconds. |

Switch weapons off in the Setup tab if you prefer a clean race. Time trial and Drift Attack
never use them.

## Tricks and style points

Drifting, air time and close passes score style points. Flight tricks join a chain while you
are in the air and are banked when you land. Landing well multiplies the chain; a crash banks
only a small fixed amount.

| Trick | Points | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air Time | 50, plus 100 a second | Stay airborne for at least 0.6 seconds |
| Barrel Roll | 400 | One full roll in the air |
| Double Barrel | 900 | Two rolls; replaces Barrel Roll in the chain |
| Front Flip | 500 | Up in the air, a full nose-over |
| Back Flip | 500 | Down in the air, a full backflip |
| Flat Spin | 350 | Shift plus Left or Right in the air, a full turn |
| 720 Spin | 900 | Two turns; replaces Flat Spin |
| Corkscrew | 1,200 | A roll and a flip in the same jump |
| Cow Vault | 600 | Fly over an animal at height |
| Perfect Start | 300 | Hold the throttle for 0.05 to 0.3 seconds during the countdown |
| Blue Turbo | 100 | Drift for about a second, then let go |
| Orange Turbo | 250 | Drift for about two seconds, then let go |
| Pink Turbo | 500 | Drift for more than three seconds, then let go |
| Slingshot | 150 | Leave a rival's slipstream at speed |
| Reverse Hero | 200, plus 100 a second | Reverse for two seconds |
| J-Turn | 400 | Go from reverse back to forward speed quickly |

Sliding sideways pays, and so do loose surfaces, drift zones and near misses. Keep a chain
going for up to eight times the points. The chain only counts when it ends.

## Camera

Press C to change the view.

| View | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Chase | Close and low behind the car (the default) |
| High | Further back and higher, so you see more of the road ahead |
| Drone | High above the car for the big picture |
| Bumper | Right at the bumper, looking far ahead |

The camera turns with the car, so holding right always moves the car to the right on screen.

## Weather

The weather changes during the race on a fixed schedule: rain in the harbour and in the city,
snow on the glacier, and fog, mist, heat and glare. It affects every car equally.

## Sound

Every sound is generated by AuraGo's own code: an engine loop for each car, impacts, animal
calls, weapon effects, and music that changes with each biome. Nothing is recorded from the
outside world and nothing is downloaded. If a sample cannot load, the game falls back to
synthesised sound.

## Settings

Volume, mute, graphics tier (auto, low, medium, high, ultra), reduced motion, the ghost display
and the weapon switch are in the menu. On auto the game adjusts the graphics tier to your frame
rate. A software renderer on auto gets the flat 2D view. Best times and settings stay in this
browser. A read-only Desktop saves nothing.

## If the window stays empty

The first release used class names such as `ad-overlay` and `ad-panel`. Ad blockers (AdGuard,
uBlock, EasyList) treat `ad-` classes as advertising and hide them with `display: none`. The
game was running but invisible, and the console stayed empty. Current versions use the
`agdrift-` prefix and are no longer affected. If it still fails, whitelist the Desktop address in
your ad blocker and reload the page.

## Boundaries

Aura Drift aims for believable, deterministic driving, not an engineering-grade vehicle
simulation. The 3D look was built to a plan and has been checked by automated tests, but it
has not yet been played by a person on a real screen; the feel, the balance and the sound are
still being judged. There is no multiplayer or online leaderboard. See the
[technical guide](../../aura-drift.md) for details.
