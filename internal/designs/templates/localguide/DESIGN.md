# LocalGuide Design System

## Overview

LocalGuide is an authentic, map-forward design system built around insider-tips and local discovery for city guide and experience platforms. Bold red-orange anchors the visual hierarchy while teal and yellow accent supporting elements.

## Colors

- **Primary Red-Orange** (#EA580C): Primary actions, key markers
- **Secondary Teal** (#0D9488): Links, secondary actions
- **Tertiary Yellow** (#EAB308): Highlights, badges, ratings
- **Background** (#FFFFFF): Page background
- **Surface Default** (#FFFFFF): Card backgrounds
- **Success** (#16A34A)
- **Warning** (#EAB308)
- **Error** (#DC2626)
- **Info** (#0D9488)

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do lead with map-based interfaces
- Don't hide key location details behind extra taps
- Do provide clear filter chips for quick category browsing
